ShellWeapon

This item only works when running on the client. Client

The client side of the shell-firing train turrets (Cannon, Burst Cannon, AutoCannon, Energy Cannon, CIWS). start(Config, packets) renders every placed turret of one weapon for all players: one TurretRig per turret in workspace.PlacedObjects (Discovery follows the PlaceableName attribute and drops the rig when the turret is removed or stashed), eased toward the replicated AimYaw / AimPitch, and the weapon's Fired shots: barrel fold (a per-turret Recoil written after the rig updates), muzzle flash, a pooled neon tracer in Config.TracerColor and the fire sound. Impact sound comes from ExplosionAt (Config.WeaponName); the reload sound plays once when Reloading turns true. Shots whose shooter is the local player while the local player mans that turret are skipped: the Gunner hooks below predict them. The tracers are client-only Parts. Gunner also gets each turret's mount prompt and its step runs first in the per-frame order. Every start call keeps its own state, pool and render step.

Heat mode (Energy Cannon: Config.HeatPerShot is set): the gunner predicts heat with EnergyCannonMath instead of ammo (resynced from the Heat / Overheated attributes on mount and on every overheat edge), there is no R reload, every client plays the overheat / vent sounds on the Overheated edges, and HeatGlow recolours the model's glow parts. With packets.Impact (no-splash bolts) each hit gets a flash and Config.HitSound.

Barrel spin (CIWS: Config.SpinRate is set, with SpinUp / SpinDown): each barrel joint also turns about its own Z while the turret fires (a shot within the last 3 x BurstPause) and spins down after. Cosmetic only.

Spread: the gunner's predicted shells roll inside SpreadBase + bloom with the same CrosshairMath rules as the server, and the mount publishes that cone as the crosshair source (cleared on dismount) for the TurretGui crosshair. The predicted tracer's random draw differs from the server shell's; the server wins.
