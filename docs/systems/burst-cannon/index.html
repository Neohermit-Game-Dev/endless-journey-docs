Burst Cannon

A train-mounted, four-barrel turret that a player mans and fires 4-shot bursts by holding the trigger. It is a grid placeable (max 2 per train), crafted at the Workbench after the TrainWep_BurstCannon research. The server owns the turret: it seats the gunner, turns the rig at a fixed rate, fires the shells from its own rig math and applies the damage. Shells are lenient: a spherecast plus a proximity fuse, and the enemy that trips the fuse (or is hit) always takes full damage.

Attributes on the placed Model: AimYaw, AimPitch (radians), Ammo, Reloading, ReloadEndsAt (workspace:GetServerTimeNow()), GunnerUserId (0 = none), plus the HUD contract DisplayName, MagazineSize, ReloadTime (set once at register from BurstCannonConfig).

How it works​

Discovery​

TrainTurretServer (the shared core that BurstCannonServer runs on; Discovery, Seating, Aim and the unmanned aim scan live in the core, the fire loop, shells and magazine in BurstCannonServer) watches workspace.PlacedObjects for Models with PlaceableName = "BurstCannon". PlacementServer sets that attribute right after parenting, so a Model that arrives without it is registered when the attribute appears (same rule as the Cannon). Each turret gets one record and one Janitor. Removing or stashing the turret unregisters it and clears the gunner.

Stash / Move. Each placed turret has a Stash (hold F) and a Move (hold G) prompt next to the mount prompt (see Gunner controls). PlaceableActionsServer refuses both while GunnerUserId names a live player. A stashed or moved turret is a new Model when it is placed again, so it comes back with a full magazine.

Seating (the server sits the player)​

  1. The client sends ReqMountBurstCannon(model).
  2. The server checks: the player is alive, the HumanoidRootPart is within PromptRange of Body.Seat, the same humanoid did not leave this seat less than SeatCooldown ago, and no other live player mans the turret (a dead gunner's turret can be taken over).
  3. The server records the gunner, sets GunnerUserId, and calls Seat:Sit(humanoid).

Body.Seat.CanTouch is false on the template (and on every build ghost), so walking into the seat does not sit you; only the server's Seat:Sit does. Only the humanoid the server seated may stay. Any other Seat.Occupant (for example from a script) is ejected on the next frame by destroying the SeatWeld. The eject waits one frame because destroying the weld while the sit is still running leaves Humanoid.SeatPart stale on the server, and then a later Seat:Sit does nothing.

The gunner is cleared when the player jumps out of the seat, dies or resets, sends ReqMountBurstCannon(nil), leaves the game, or the turret is removed. Clearing stops the trigger and sets GunnerUserId to 0. A fresh mount (a new gunner or a new character) has 0.5 s to appear in Seat.Occupant; a repeated request from the same gunner does not extend that window, and the turret never fires while the gunner is not seated.

Seat cooldown

Roblox does not let a character sit in the same seat for about 3 s after it leaves. The server records the humanoid that last left the seat and when (from Seat.Occupant changes). A mount by that same humanoid within SeatCooldown (3 s) is rejected, so the client never shows the HUD. A different player may mount at once.

Aim​

The gunner's client reports ReqBurstCannonAim(yaw, pitch, dir) at AimReportHz over an unreliable packet. Reports from anyone but the current gunner, and reports with a non-finite yaw or pitch, are ignored. The yaw and pitch are the targets: every Heartbeat the server steps its own angles toward them by at most YawSpeed × dt (the short way round) and PitchSpeed × dt, and clamps pitch to PitchMin..PitchMax. It then sets Base.Yaw.Transform and Body.Frame.Pitch.Transform on the server, so the seat and the seated gunner are where the clients draw them. AimYaw/AimPitch are re-published only when they change by more than 0.002 rad, at most 20 times a second.

The aim direction (from aim reports and trigger presses) is stored as the last valid direction; NaN, infinite or zero directions are ignored.

Fire loop​

While the trigger is held (or a tap has not been served yet, or a burst is still running), the gunner is live and seated (Seat.Occupant is the seated humanoid), the turret is not reloading and Ammo > 0, the server fires bursts on Heartbeat: BurstSize (4) shells, one per barrel, 1 / BurstRate (0.05 s) apart, then BurstPause (0.9 s) before the next burst. A started burst finishes after the trigger is released (it stops early only at an empty magazine, a reload or the gunner leaving). The cadence comes from the shared BurstCannonMath.afterShot, which the client prediction also uses:

  1. Next barrel in BarrelOrder (TR, BL, TL, BR).
  2. Origin = BurstCannonMath.muzzle(jointFrame(Base.CFrame * Yaw.C0, Yaw.C1:Inverse() * Pitch.C0, yaw, pitch), MuzzleTips[key]) — the server's own angles, never the client's origin.
  3. Direction = clampToCone(lastAimDir, barrelDir, AimConeDeg), or the barrel direction when there is no valid aim.
  4. SpherecastFire(origin, ShellRadius, dir, ShellSpeed) with Acceleration = Gravity × GravityScale, MaxDistance = MaxRange, excluding the turret and the gunner's character.
  5. BurstCannonFired(model, barrelIndex, origin, velocity, shooter) to all clients.
  6. Ammo -= 1; at 0 a reload starts.

A trigger press that is shorter than one server frame still fires one shot (the tapped latch). A press during a reload is not latched.

Lenient hits​

Each cast step (LengthChanged):

  • Rocks: RockService.SegmentHit on the step; a rock detonates the shell there.
  • Proximity fuse: the step segment is tested against every live enemy with closestOnSegment to the enemy's HumanoidRootPart (or pivot). The enemy list and their points (TrainWeaponDamage.liveEnemies() + targetPoint) are cached once per frame and shared by every shell and the unmanned scan. The closest enemy within FuseRadius detonates the shell at the closest point on the segment.

Hit detonates at the hit point; if the hit part belongs to an enemy model (TrainWeaponDamage.enemyOf), that model is the direct target.

Detonation runs once per shell (the done guard): applyDirect(target) gives the fused or hit enemy the full damage, then applyBlast(center, BlastRadius, damage, shooter, ignore ∪ {target}) splashes the others with linear falloff (the target, Humanoid or EnemyShip, is excluded, so it is not hit twice), then ExplosionAt(center, BlastRadius, "BurstCannon"). Damage is Damage × (1 + PerkBuffRead.Party("CannonDamageBonus")), read per shot. A shell that reaches MaxRange does nothing.

Why full damage: CombatService.ApplyAOE falls off from the enemy pivot to 0 at the blast edge, so a 5-stud fuse with a 4-stud blast would turn near-misses into 0-damage hits.

Unmanned auto-fire (Smart Cannon / Orbital Sentinel)​

While PerkBuffRead.Party("AutoLoaderRate") > 0 and its holder is in the game, a turret with no gunner for CannonConfig.AutoLoaderIdleTime (3 s) fires by itself. The holder is OrbitalSentinelHolderId when it is not 0, else AutoLoaderHolderId (same rule as CannonServer). The idle clock (mannedAt) runs from the last frame a gunner was set, and a new turret starts idle.

  • Every 0.5 s it scans TrainWeaponDamage.liveEnemies() for the nearest enemy within CannonConfig.AutoLoaderRange (150) whose aim point is inside the pitch limits and has a clear raycast from the pitch pivot (only the turret is ignored). A tracked enemy that dies or is removed is dropped at once.
  • Each Heartbeat it sets the target yaw/pitch with BurstCannonMath.aimAngles; the normal turn-rate stepping and attribute publishing move the rig, so every client sees it turn.
  • It fires through the same fireShot path (same magazine, reload, fuse, blast) in the same bursts with the cadence scaled by AutoLoaderRate (gaps and pause 3.3x longer for Smart Cannon, 1.7x for Orbital Sentinel) once the barrels are within AimConeDeg of the target. The shell flies along the barrel (no client aim), and the holder is the shooter, so damage and kill credit go to the holder.
  • It stops on the next frame when someone mounts (rec.gunner is set), when the holder leaves or the perk ends, or when no enemy is in range and visible. After a dismount it waits AutoLoaderIdleTime again.
  • The aim point ignores shell drop (about 1 stud at 150 studs); the fuse radius covers it.

Magazine and reload​

Ammo starts at MagazineSize. At 0 the turret reloads for ReloadTime: Reloading = true, ReloadEndsAt = GetServerTimeNow() + ReloadTime; at the end Ammo = MagazineSize and Reloading = false. ReqBurstCannonReload from the gunner starts a reload when 0 < Ammo < MagazineSize. Ammo is free. Stashing and placing the turret again also refills the magazine (a new Model registers with MagazineSize).

Server authority & anti-exploit​

  • Origin always comes from the server's rig math; the client direction is clamped into the aim cone around the barrel.
  • Turn rate and pitch limits are enforced by the server's own stepping; a client cannot snap the turret.
  • Only the seated gunner's aim, trigger and reload packets are used.
  • The server seats the gunner itself and ejects every other occupant.
  • Stash and move are refused while a live gunner mans the turret.
  • No server-replicated shell Parts: clients draw tracers from BurstCannonFired.

Client rig and shot FX (all players)​

BurstCannonController keeps one TurretRig per Model in workspace.PlacedObjects with PlaceableName = "BurstCannon". A Model that arrives without the attribute is picked up when it appears; a removed or stashed turret drops its rig (one Janitor per turret). Each frame (BindToRenderStep("BurstCannonRigs", Camera + 4), after the Gunner's step at Camera + 3) TurretRig writes Transform on Base.Yaw and Body.Frame.Pitch, then the controller's Recoil writes every Slide (Studio edit mode does not apply Motor6D.Transform, play mode does).

  • Aim: a new rig snaps to the current AimYaw / AimPitch (a late joiner sees no sweep from 0), then eases (exponential, 12 per second) toward them. A rig with External = true is not steered by the attributes: the local gunner's predictor drives it with rig:SetAim(yaw, pitch, true). Other scripts reach a rig with TurretRig.get(model). The controller finds the joints (WaitForChild, 10 s timeout) and passes them to TurretRig.new, which does not yield.
  • Fold: each barrel folds by max(foldAt(time since its shot), reloadFold). While Reloading is true, all four barrels fold and wind out over ReloadTime; the time comes from ReloadEndsAt and workspace:GetServerTimeNow().
  • Shot (BurstCannonFired): the barrel fold, a 0.05 s neon muzzle flash, a green neon tracer that flies at the packet velocity with the shell gravity for MaxRange / ShellSpeed seconds, and FireSound at the muzzle. Flash and tracer are pooled GenericPoolInstance Parts that exist only on the client. A shot is skipped when its shooter is the local player and GunnerUserId is the local player, because the local gunner predicted it. An unmanned (Smart Cannon) shot names the perk holder as shooter while GunnerUserId is 0, so the holder's client still draws it.
  • Sounds: ExplosionAt with "BurstCannon" plays ExplodeSound at the blast (the visual comes from WeaponEffectsController). ReloadSound plays once when Reloading turns true. None of the three replicate; every client plays them itself. Sound ids in SoundConfig are blank until filled.

The tracer is cosmetic and flies the full range: it does not stop at an enemy that the server fused on.

Gunner controls (local player)​

TrainTurret/Gunner.luau runs the local player's side for every train turret. One module instance serves all turret controllers (one mount at a time); init.client.luau calls Gunner.attach(model, promptPart, cleaner, def) with a GunnerDef (Config, the three packets ReqMountBurstCannon / ReqBurstCannonTrigger / ReqBurstCannonAim, and the hooks CanTap = not Reloading, OnMount, OnKey (R sends ReqBurstCannonReload), OnStep (the predicted-shot block), OnDismount). Gunner binds its own per-frame step once with BindToRenderStep("TrainTurretGunner", Camera + 3); the controllers' rig loops run at Camera + 4.

  • Prompts: each placed turret gets a client-made mount ProximityPrompt on Body.MountPromptPart (PromptActionText, PromptObjectText, MaxActivationDistance = PromptRange, no line of sight), plus a Stash (hold F) and a Move (hold G) prompt from PlaceableActions.addPrompts with the same range (hold time PlacementConfig.PlaceableActions.PromptHoldDuration, 0.6 s). They use the same request path as the Workbench buttons (PlaceableActions.request). All three are hidden while GunnerUserId is not 0, and while the local player mans any turret (an BurstCannon or a Cannon, via OnTurretMounted / OnCannonMounted), so no turret shows prompts to a seated gunner.
  • Mount: the prompt sends ReqMountBurstCannon(model). On true the client unequips tools (a held gun would fire too and owns the aim camera), fires Signals.RequestShootingCamera(true) (the CameraController aim mode: mouse locked to the centre, reticle shown, same gate PlacementClient uses) and fires Signals.OnTurretMounted(model). While mounted, a tool equipped from the hotbar is unequipped on the next frame. The unequip makes the weapon or GrabHook send RequestShootingCamera(false) (last writer wins), so when OnShootingCameraToggled(false) arrives the client defers a new RequestShootingCamera(true).
  • Aim (every frame, before the rigs update): the camera-centre ray to MaxRange (turret and own character excluded; enemy parts have CanCollide off, so the ray keeps the default and hits any CanQuery part) gives the aim point. The target angles are aimAngles(Base.CFrame * Yaw.C0, aimPoint - pitch pivot); the local yaw/pitch step toward them at YawSpeed/PitchSpeed (pitch clamped to PitchMin..PitchMax) and go to the local rig with rig.External = true and SetAim(yaw, pitch, true). ReqBurstCannonAim(yaw, pitch, aimDir) goes out at AimReportHz; aimDir is the unit vector from the pitch pivot to the aim point.
  • Fire: Gunner.SetTrigger(down) is the single trigger path (MouseButton1, plus the touch FIRE button through Signals.TurretTrigger, which the mount session's Janitor connects to SetTrigger). It sends ReqBurstCannonTrigger(down, aimDir). While held (or a tap not yet served), seated, Ammo > 0 and not Reloading, the client predicts a shot every 1 / FireRate: next barrel, origin and barrel direction from muzzle(jointFrame(local yaw, pitch), tip), direction clampToCone(aimDir, barrelDir, AimConeDeg), then the same fold + flash + tracer + sound as a broadcast shot. A press during a reload is not latched (same as the server). A local round count gates the prediction (min(Ammo, local), minus one per predicted shot, refilled when Reloading turns false), so no extra tracers are drawn at the end of a magazine before Ammo = 0 arrives. Losing window focus releases the trigger.
  • Keys: R sends ReqBurstCannonReload; X leaves.
  • Leave: X, death, the turret leaving the workspace, or GunnerUserId changing away from the local player (this covers a jump-off, a failed sit inside the seat cooldown, an eject and a takeover). Leaving destroys the mount Janitor: aim mode off (RequestShootingCamera(false)), rig.External = false, Sit = false if still seated, OnTurretMounted(nil), then ReqMountBurstCannon(nil). Walk speed and jump power are never changed.

The server's answer and GunnerUserId are the source of truth; the client never decides it is mounted from Humanoid.SeatPart alone.

Gunner HUD (generic TurretGui)​

StarterGui.TurretGui (ScreenGui, Enabled = false, ResetOnSpawn = false, IgnoreGuiInset = true, DisplayOrder = 130 so the touch FIRE button sits above the Satchel hotbar's 120) and TurretGuiController.client.luau contain nothing BurstCannon-specific. Any future turret reuses them: fire Signals.OnTurretMounted(model) on mount and nil on leave, and publish this attribute contract on the turret Model.

AttributeTypeUse
DisplayNamestringPanel title. Missing: model.Name:upper()
AmmonumberCount and bar fill
MagazineSizenumberMax count. Missing: the count row, bar and the R RELOAD hint part are hidden
ReloadingbooleanSwaps the count for RELOADING and the bar to the reload fill

The HUD shows only while mounted. The crosshair sits at the exact screen centre Gunner aims at (ViewportSize / 2, so the ScreenGui ignores the GUI inset). Ammo at or below 15 turns the count and fill amber, 0 turns the count red. Desktop (not TouchEnabled) shows the X LEAVE | R RELOAD | LMB FIRE hint (key caps 28 px, text 15 px). Touch shows a hold-to-fire button that fires Signals.TurretTrigger(true/false); touch leaves with the jump button. While mounted the controller also disables the CameraController's AimReticle ScreenGui (its ring would sit about 29 px off the crosshair) and re-enables it on leave. On touch the ammo panel moves up and FIRE sits left of the native jump button and above the Satchel hotbar; those corner offsets scale with the UIScale so they keep clear of the jump button on large screens. FIRE is Active = false while reloading, and a press on it is ignored then. The per-turret attribute connections live in a Janitor that is cleaned on every OnTurretMounted change, and the bar is only stepped on RenderStepped while the HUD is visible.

Heat mode (a turret with MaxHeat, the Energy Cannon) hides the ammo panel and the R RELOAD hint part, so the hint reads X LEAVE | LMB FIRE. One lean HeatBar (420 x 18, phone 150 x 12) sits 28 px above the hint on desktop and 30 px above the FIRE button on touch. Its fill eases over one 0.1 s step with MotionMaster (heat arrives at about 10 Hz). The glow, sweep and overheat blink are written per frame on one RenderStepped that is bound only while one of them is visible; it lives in the per-mount Janitor. Heat mode adds no percent readout, marker or panel. The prototype is roblox-gui-prototypes/turret-heat-hud.html.

Data flow​

sequenceDiagram
    participant Cl as Gunner client
    participant Sv as BurstCannonServer + TrainTurretServer
    participant All as All clients

    Cl->>Sv: ReqMountBurstCannon(model)
    Sv->>Sv: alive, PromptRange from Seat, single live gunner
    Sv->>Sv: GunnerUserId = player, Seat:Sit(humanoid)
    Sv-->>Cl: accepted

    loop AimReportHz
        Cl-)Sv: ReqBurstCannonAim(yaw, pitch, dir) - unreliable
    end
    Sv->>Sv: Heartbeat: step yaw/pitch at turn rate, set Yaw/Pitch Transform, AimYaw/AimPitch

    Cl->>Sv: ReqBurstCannonTrigger(true, dir)
    loop bursts of BurstSize while held and Ammo > 0
        Sv->>Sv: origin from rig math, dir clamped to aim cone, SpherecastFire
        Sv->>All: BurstCannonFired(model, barrel, origin, velocity, shooter)
        Note over Sv: fuse within FuseRadius or Hit
        Sv->>Sv: applyDirect(target) full damage, applyBlast(others)
        Sv->>All: ExplosionAt(center, BlastRadius, "BurstCannon")
    end
    Cl->>Sv: ReqBurstCannonTrigger(false, dir)
    Sv->>Sv: Ammo 0: Reloading, ReloadEndsAt; after ReloadTime Ammo = MagazineSize
