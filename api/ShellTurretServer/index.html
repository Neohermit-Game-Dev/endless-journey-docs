ShellTurretServer

This item only works when running on the server. Server

The shared weapon layer of the shell-firing train turrets (Cannon, Burst Cannon, AutoCannon, Energy Cannon, CIWS). start(Config, packets) runs one weapon on the shared turret core (TrainTurretServer), which discovers placed turrets in workspace.PlacedObjects, seats the gunner after validation (and ejects anyone it did not seat) and steps the yaw/pitch toward the gunner's aim reports at the turn-rate limit. This module adds the magazine and reload, and FastCast2 spherecast shells fired from the turret's own rig math in bursts of BurstSize (BurstSize = 1 is plain full-auto). A proximity fuse and a direct hit both give the target full damage (TrainWeaponDamage.applyDirect) before the splash (applyBlast). State is published as attributes on the placed Model: AimYaw, AimPitch, Ammo, Reloading, ReloadEndsAt, GunnerUserId, plus the HUD contract DisplayName, MagazineSize, ReloadTime, AmmoType (read by the generic TurretGui).

Reloads draw from the party's TrainAmmoPool (Config.AmmoType): a placed turret starts empty, reloads on its own whenever the magazine is empty and the pool has rounds, takes the rounds when the reload ends (a short pool gives a partial magazine), and gives its loaded rounds back when it is removed.

With no gunner for CannonConfig.AutoLoaderIdleTime and a Smart Cannon / Orbital Sentinel holder in the party, the turret aims at the nearest visible enemy itself and fires at AutoLoaderRate x the manned cadence, credited to the holder. A started burst finishes after the trigger is released.

Heat mode (Energy Cannon, Burst Cannon: Config.HeatPerShot is set) replaces the magazine and reload with EnergyCannonMath heat: each shot adds heat, the shot that reaches max starts an overheat lock, and the turret publishes Heat, Overheated, MaxHeat, PeakHeat instead of Ammo / MagazineSize (the TurretGui heat mode). Unmanned fire keeps out of overheat with EnergyCannonMath.autoAllowed. No-splash shots (Energy Cannon, CIWS: Config.BlastRadius == 0) damage only what they hit (an enemy through applyDirect, a rock through RockService.Damage) and broadcast packets.Impact instead of ExplosionAt.

Spread: every shell (manned or unmanned) rolls its direction inside SpreadBase + bloom (CrosshairMath, WeaponValidation.applySpread) after the aim-cone clamp; each shot adds SpreadPerShot (capped at SpreadMax), decaying at SpreadRecovery deg/s. The gunner client mirrors the bloom for its crosshair.
