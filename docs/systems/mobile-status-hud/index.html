Mobile Status HUD

Phone players get one slim strip instead of the PC HealthStaminaGui and TrainFuelGui: HP (+ shield), stamina and train fuel side by side, centred just above the Satchel hotbar. It collapses to a small expand pill when nothing needs attention and opens itself when something does.

Files: StarterPlayerScripts/MobileStatusGuiController.client.luau (drives the GUI), SharedModules/MobileStatusLogic.luau (pure open/close rules + selfCheck), SharedModules/MobileHud.luau (phone gate), Configs/MobileStatusConfig.luau (timings, thresholds, position), StarterGui.MobileStatusGui (authored in Studio).

Who gets it​

MobileHud.IsActive() = TouchEnabled and not KeyboardEnabled, read once at startup (the same rule as the mobile sprint button). When true, MobileStatusGuiController enables the strip and the two PC HUD controllers leave their ScreenGui off. Each controller reads the gate itself, so script start order cannot leave the PC panels on. A small PC window keeps the PC HUD (its compact layouts).

What it shows​

GaugeDataStates
HPSignals.OnHealthValueChanged(health, shield), max = humanoid.MaxHealthshield line under the bar while shield > 0; critical (≤ 25 %) pulses the strip outline red/rust
StaminaSignals.OnStaminaValueChanged, max = MaxStamina × (1 + MaxStaminaBonus)TIRED in amber at 0
FuelTrainStateCache.PredictedFuel() / GetFuel(), eased like TrainFuelGuiamber fill while braked; coast: countdown (12s) amber + orange outline; stalled: 0% + red/rust pulse; no snapshot: —

Each bar is 10 cells; cell i fills clamp(frac * 10 - (i - 1), 0, 1).

Open / close rules​

EventEffect
Join / respawn / reviveStarts collapsed (only the expand pill); opens at once if a hold is true
Expand buttonOpen 30 s (ManualOpenSeconds)
Collapse buttonCloses at once; stays closed until a hold newly starts or a hit
Hit (HP or shield lost, HP > 0)Open 3 s (HitOpenSeconds); each hit restarts it
Fuel shortage (< 25 % of max, coast, or stall)Opens and stays open; 30 s more after it ends
Critical HP (≤ 25 %)Opens and stays open; 30 s more after it ends
Sprinting with stamina < 30 %Opens and stays open; 3 s more after it ends
Dead / spectating / waiting for reviveHidden (ReviveStateSync status ≠ Alive, or HP 0)

The strip closes at the latest pending close time, never while a hold is true. A fuel snapshot that has not arrived yet is not a shortage.

Run the rules' self-check from the Studio command bar: print(loadstring(game.ReplicatedStorage.SharedModules.MobileStatusLogic.Source)().selfCheck()).

Layout​

The strip's bottom edge sits HotbarGap (6 px) above Satchel's Hotbar frame (both ScreenGuis ignore the topbar inset), or BottomOffset (78 px) above the screen bottom if the hotbar is not found. UIScale uses the PC HUDs' compact maths: clamp(min(W / 844, H / 390), 0.75, 1.5). The strip and the pill swap with a 0.2 s MotionMaster grow. The collapse chevron and the pill each sit in a transparent 44 px tall hit button.

Icons: heart, bolt and chevron PNGs in roblox-gui-prototypes/assets/mobile-status/ (set the uploaded ids on Strip.Health.Icon, Strip.Stamina.Icon, Strip.CollapseSlot.Chevron, ExpandPill.Content.Heart and ExpandPill.Content.Chevron); fuel reuses the train icon.

Build mode on touch disables MobileStatusGui with the other HUDs (BuildModeGuiController.HIDDEN_WHILE_BUILDING) and restores it on exit.

Known gap: TurretGui.Hint / HeatBar also sit bottom-centre while a turret is mounted; the turret hint moves in a separate change.
