Cannon

A train-mounted single-barrel cannon that a player mans and fires by holding the trigger. It is the tier 1 train weapon: a grid placeable (max 2 per train), crafted at the Workbench after the TrainWep_Cannon research (the AutoCannon and Energy Cannon nodes need it). It fires at a medium rate: one shell every 1.2 s, a yellow tracer, a small blast.

The Cannon runs on exactly the same code as the Burst Cannon and the AutoCannon: ShellTurretServer on the server and TrainTurret/ShellWeapon on the client, each started with CannonConfig and the Cannon packets. Mounting, seating, aim, the fire loop, lenient shells (fuse + direct hit), magazine and reload, unmanned Smart Cannon / Orbital Sentinel fire, Stash / Move prompts and the generic TurretGui all work as described on the Burst Cannon page. This page lists only what differs.

Packets: ReqMountCannon, ReqCannonTrigger, ReqCannonReload, ReqCannonAim (unreliable), CannonFired (barrel index always 1); impact reuses ExplosionAt with "Cannon". See Networking Map.

Attributes: the same contract as the Burst Cannon (AimYaw, AimPitch, Ammo, Reloading, ReloadEndsAt, GunnerUserId, DisplayName, MagazineSize, ReloadTime).

What differs from the Burst Cannon​

  • One barrel. BarrelOrder = { "C" }, so every shot leaves Gun.Barrel_C. SleeveStroke = 0: the barrel folds alone, with a heavy kick (BarrelStroke 1.0, FoldOut 0.4 s).
  • Cadence. BurstSize = 1, so every shot is its own burst and the shared afterShot waits BurstPause (1.2 s) after every shot.
  • Slower aim. YawSpeed 90 °/s and PitchSpeed 60 °/s (the AutoCannon turns at 120 / 90).
  • Small blast. BlastRadius 3, FuseRadius 4.
  • Colours. TracerColor yellow (255, 205, 50), FlashColor (255, 225, 130).
  • Balance. Sustained single-target DPS = Damage × MagazineSize / ((MagazineSize − 1) × BurstPause + ReloadTime) = 70 × 8 / (8.4 + 3.5) = 47: below every tier 2+ turret (Energy Cannon best play about 91, AutoCannon 100). SturmlanzeMath.selfCheck asserts that order.

Shared constants​

Every train turret reads these from CannonConfig, so they stay here even though they are not Cannon-only:

  • AutoLoaderIdleTime (3 s) / AutoLoaderRange (150 studs): Smart Cannon / Orbital Sentinel unmanned fire (TrainTurretServer, ShellTurretServer).
  • ENEMY_SHIP_TAG ("EnemyShip"): non-Humanoid destructibles that take train-weapon damage through a numeric Health attribute (TrainWeaponDamage, WreckSalvageService). Inert until a future enemy-ship spec tags ships.

Rig (template)​

  • Base (anchored PrimaryPart, union: the AutoCannon pedestal with four short legs) → Base.Yaw Motor6D → Body.Frame (union: yaw ring, saddle, tapered trunnion cheeks, left seat pod, sight).
  • Body.Frame.Pitch Motor6D (at the trunnion) → Gun.GunBody (union: cradle, armored top with vent slots, breech, recuperator).
  • Gun.Barrel_C (union, real bore + windowed box muzzle brake) with a Slide Motor6D from GunBody (+Z = back) and a MuzzleTip attachment at (0, 0, −12.4) in the pitch-joint frame (= MuzzleTips.C).
  • Body.Seat (CanTouch = false), Body.MountPromptPart, CollisionBox (10.4 × 7.1 × 10.4, covers the seat's sweep).
  • Neon and joystick details are plain Parts welded to their union (no collision, query or touch). Do not merge them into unions: union LOD makes thin neon parts float.
  • The gun pitches from −15° to +45°; at +45° the breech passes just behind the saddle.

Tuning​

Config module: CannonConfig. Perk CannonDamageBonus multiplies shell damage, as for the Burst Cannon. There is no heat, so CannonHeatReduction does not affect the Cannon.
