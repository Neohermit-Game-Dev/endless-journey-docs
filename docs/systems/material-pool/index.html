Material Pool

Server-wide, session-only material pool (Wood, Metal, and Energy points) shared by every player, plus the top-right Materials HUD.

At a glance​

LayerFileRole
Serversrc/ServerStorage/SharedModules/MaterialPool.luauSole authority: Get, atomic Spend, SpendUpTo, Add, ArcPoints; broadcasts every change; "MaterialPool" JoinSync sender
Sharedsrc/ReplicatedStorage/SharedModules/MaterialPoolClient.luauClient read model: Get (nil before first snapshot), Changed(gained, origin, landing), idempotent Init
Clientsrc/StarterPlayer/StarterPlayerScripts/MaterialHudController.client.luauCode-built HUD, slide/toggle, fly-to-HUD effect
Configsrc/ReplicatedStorage/SharedModules/Configs/MaterialHudConfig.luauHUD + effect tuning

Packets: MaterialPoolUpdate — see Networking Map

How it works​

  • Sources: shredder completion (ShredderServer) and research cancel refunds (ResearchService) call MaterialPool.Add(counts, MaterialPool.ArcPoints(station)).
  • Sinks: workbench crafts (WorkbenchService) and research enqueues (ResearchService) call MaterialPool.Spend(cost); false means nothing was consumed.
  • SpendUpTo(mat, missing, perPoint): the Energy spend path. Spends ceil(missing ÷ perPoint) points (at least 1), capped by the pool, and returns (pointsSpent, unitsAdded). Every Energy consumer calls it with its own *_PER_POINT rate, so a top-up never wastes points.
  • Replication: every change fires MaterialPoolUpdate(wood, metal, energy, gainedWood, gainedMetal, gainedEnergy, origin, landing) to all clients. Spends and the join snapshot carry zero gain. The join snapshot is a broadcast, not FireClient, because the Packet library flushes broadcasts first each frame — a per-player snapshot could land after a same-frame gain and leave the joiner stale.
  • Effect backstop: each fly effect destroys its Janitor after its time budget even if a piece thread errors, so undelivered value is always handed back to the counter.
  • HUD: hidden by default. Slides down on any gain, on LeftControl, or on the touch-only pull tab; tapping the open panel or pressing the key again closes it; auto-hides after HIDE_DELAY.
  • Rows: Wood, Metal, Energy (Energy colour 13FAFD). Panel sized for three rows: PANEL_HEIGHT_SCALE 0.165, PANEL_MIN_HEIGHT_PX 96, PANEL_ASPECT 1.6.
  • Effect: if the gain's origin is on screen and within MAX_EFFECT_DISTANCE, up to MAX_FLY_PIECES local Handle clones arc out, rest for LAND_HOLD, then fly as 2D icons into their row; each carries a share of the gain. Pieces are split across materials in proportion to the gain; every material with a gain gets at least one. Energy has no CraftingMaterials template, so its pieces skip the 3D arc and fly as 2D icons from the grab point. Otherwise the counter rolls up over ROLL_DURATION.
  • Counter rule: displayed = server total − value still in flight (clamped ≥ 0), so overlapping gains and mid-flight spends always settle on the exact total.

Data flow​

sequenceDiagram
    participant SS as ShredderServer / ResearchService
    participant MP as MaterialPool (server)
    participant C as MaterialPoolClient (every client)
    participant H as MaterialHudController
    participant G as Workbench / TechTree GUIs

    SS->>MP: Add({Wood, Metal}, origin, landing)
    MP->>C: MaterialPoolUpdate(wood, metal, energy, gainedWood, gainedMetal, gainedEnergy, origin, landing)
    C->>H: Changed(gained, origin, landing)
    C->>G: Changed (repaint if open)
    H->>H: slide down, arc + fly-to-HUD, tick counters, hide after delay
    Note over MP: WorkbenchService / ResearchService call Spend (atomic)

Tuning​

MaterialHudConfig: TOGGLE_KEY, HIDE_DELAY, SLIDE_DURATION, PANEL_HEIGHT_SCALE, PANEL_MIN_HEIGHT_PX, PANEL_ASPECT, MARGIN_PX, TAB_SIZE_PX, Colors, MAX_FLY_PIECES, PIECE_STAGGER, LAND_HOLD, FLY_DURATION, FLY_ICON_PX, POP_IMPULSE, ROLL_DURATION, MAX_EFFECT_DISTANCE. Arc shape: ShredderConfig.ARC_*, LANDING_SCATTER_RADIUS. Collect sound: SoundConfig.Sfx.MaterialCollect.
