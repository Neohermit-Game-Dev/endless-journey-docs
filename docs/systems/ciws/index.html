CIWS

A train-mounted, Phalanx-style rotary gun that a player mans and fires by holding the trigger. It is the tier 3 turret of the physical line: a grid placeable (max 2 per train), crafted at the Workbench after the TrainWep_CIWS research (needs TrainWep_AutoCannon; the tier 4 Schwarmfeuer needs this node). It puts a fast stream of rounds on one target: one round every 0.06 s (~16.7 per second), each a direct hit with no splash, from a 200-round magazine with a 5 s reload fed by the party's train ammo. The six-barrel cluster spins while it fires.

The CIWS runs on exactly the same code as the Burst Cannon and the AutoCannon: ShellTurretServer on the server and TrainTurret/ShellWeapon on the client, each started with CIWSConfig and the CIWS packets. Mounting, seating, aim, the fire loop, magazine and reload, unmanned Smart Cannon / Orbital Sentinel fire, Stash / Move prompts and the generic TurretGui (ammo mode) all work as described on those pages. This page lists only what differs.

What differs from the AutoCannon​

  • Train ammo. Reloads draw CIWSRound rounds from the party's train ammo pool (AmmoType). A placed turret starts empty and reloads on its own whenever the magazine is empty and the pool has rounds; the rounds are taken when the reload ends, so a short pool gives a partial magazine. Stash / Move gives the loaded rounds back. Crafted as CraftCIWSRounds (x200), hidden until this turret's research node is done.
  • Cadence. BurstSize = 1, BurstPause = 0.06 s: one round every 0.06 s while held.
  • One barrel key. BarrelOrder = { "C" }: every round leaves the cluster centre (MuzzleTips.C). The whole six-barrel cluster is Gun.Barrel_C with one Slide joint.
  • No splash. BlastRadius = 0 puts ShellTurretServer on its no-splash path (like the Energy Cannon): each round hits one enemy within FuseRadius (1.5) of its aim point or one rock (RockService.Damage), and the server sends CIWSImpact(position) instead of ExplosionAt. Every client draws a small flash and plays HitSound there.
  • Barrel spin. SpinRate = 40 rad/s, SpinUp = 0.3 s, SpinDown = 1 s. While the turret fired within the last 3 x BurstPause, ShellWeapon spins the cluster up (BurstCannonMath.stepSpin) and writes kick * CFrame.Angles(0, 0, spinAngle) on the Slide joint; after release it spins down. Cosmetic only: the server has no spin-up delay.
  • Small kick. BarrelStroke = 0.1, FoldIn = 0.02 s, FoldOut = 0.03 s, so the fold settles inside one 0.06 s gap.
  • Fast flat rounds. ShellSpeed = 700, GravityScale = 0, ShellRadius = 0.5. Amber tracers (255, 170, 60), flash (255, 220, 140). Tracer life is 600 / 700 s, so two guns firing keep about 29 tracers alive, inside the shared FX pool (24 initial, 64 max).
  • Balance. Sustained single-target DPS = Damage × MagazineSize / ((MagazineSize − 1) × BurstPause + ReloadTime) = 14 × 200 / (199 × 0.06 + 5) ≈ 165: above the energy tier 3 Burst Cannon (150 best play), below the Sturmlanze (290 best play). SturmlanzeMath.selfCheck asserts that order, the fold-vs-cadence rule and the tracer count.

Rig (template)​

Built in Studio (v6), ~12 × 12 × 19 studs, 39 parts; the model pivot is upright at the floor.

  • Base (anchored PrimaryPart, union: bolted pedestal) → Base.Yaw Motor6D (2.1 studs above the floor, world-aligned) → Body.Frame.
  • Body.Frame (union): turntable, yoke arms with flush armour plates and axle hubs, rear cabinet, left-side seat outrigger with armrests and stick plates. Body.Seat (CanTouch = false), Body.MountPromptPart. Frame.Pitch Motor6D (axle 6.6 studs above the floor) → Gun.GunBody.
  • Gun.GunBody (union, pitches): receiver, faceted octagon dome with three visor recesses, sensor pod, ammo can under the receiver, belt guide rails, feeder housing on the right. Neon details VisorGlow x3, LensGlow, ReceiverGlow x2, AmmoGlow, FeedGlow; 8 brass BeltRound parts lie parallel to the barrels.
  • Gun.Barrel_C (union: six bored barrels, spindle, ribbed rotor shroud, clamps; MuzzleGlow) with its Slide Motor6D (parented under Barrel_C, Part0 = GunBody, joint on the barrel axis, +Z = back, so spin is about the joint's Z) and a MuzzleTip attachment at the cluster centre front: (0, 0, −13.95) in the pitch-joint frame (= MuzzleTips.C).
  • CollisionBox (anchored, no collide) over the whole footprint including the seat.
  • Detail parts are plain Parts welded to their union (no collision, query, touch or shadow). Design rules for later edits: decor sits flush and follows its part's outline; visible ammo points the way it fires; an entry point needs a housing with a mouth.

Tuning​

Config module: CIWSConfig. Perk CannonDamageBonus multiplies round damage, as for the other shell turrets. Perk CannonReloadReduction (Gun Crew, Shell Pack) shortens each reload (PerkBuffRead.Reduce(ReloadTime, r)); the server writes the real length to the ReloadTime attribute at reload start, so the HUD bar and recoil fold match.

Spread​

The server rolls every shell (manned and unmanned) inside a cone of SpreadBase + bloom after the aim-cone clamp (ShellTurretServer, CrosshairMath, WeaponValidation.applySpread). Each shot adds SpreadPerShot (capped at SpreadMax); the bloom decays at SpreadRecovery degrees/s. The gunner client mirrors the bloom with its predicted shots and the TurretGui crosshair shows that cone (see Crosshair).
