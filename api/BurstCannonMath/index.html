BurstCannonMath

Pure math shared by the server (ShellTurretServer) and the client (TrainTurret/ShellWeapon) so both sides agree on where the barrels point and where a shell leaves the muzzle. Serves the Burst Cannon and the AutoCannon: the config-bound helpers take an optional cfg (default BurstCannonConfig). Angles are radians. Yaw is positive to the left, pitch is positive up, barrels look along the joint frame's -Z.
