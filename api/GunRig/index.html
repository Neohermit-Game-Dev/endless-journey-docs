GunRig

Shared formulas of the gun rig (server: ToolGripService, EnemyGear; client: the pose-editor preview and the per-frame driver in AimPoseController). A gun-rig weapon hangs from the UpperTorso on the Motor6D UpperTorso.GunJoint (Part1 = the gun's Handle, C1 = identity). Its rest frame (C0) puts the Handle where the hold places it, relative to the right shoulder and scaled by the arm reach. The animation moves the gun through the joint's Transform; the arm IK (ArmIK) pins the right hand to the gun's grip point (Tool.Grip, Handle space) and the left hand to the gun's LeftHandIK part (joint Handle.LeftHandIK, rest = the left support point). Zero pose = the old HoldAnimConfig.RightHand / LeftHand places, exactly.

Motor6D?,
Motor6D?

Server: joins handle (in container, the Tool or the pirate's EnemyWeapon model) to rig.UpperTorso with the GunJoint Motor6D, and adds the LeftHandIK part + joint under the Handle. Reuses a LeftHandIK part the container already has (re-equip). Returns both joints, or nil when the rig cannot be measured. The caller destroys GunJoint when the gun leaves the rig (it lives in the UpperTorso).
