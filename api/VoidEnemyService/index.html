VoidEnemyService

This item only works when running on the server. Server

The five flying void enemies. Spawns their invisible bodies (ServerStorage.EnemyAssets.<Id>), moves only the body root, runs one 10 Hz behavior director, and routes every enemy hit through Impact (the shield rule). Looks and visuals are client-side (VoidEnemyController). See docs/systems/void-enemies.md.

Impact​

</>
VoidEnemyService.Impact(
origin: Vector3,
target: Vector3,
attack: Attack,
attacker: Model?,
spareHull: boolean?
) → (
Vector3,
boolean
)

One enemy hit from origin toward target. Shield above 0: the shield takes Damage at the point where the segment meets it (players and deck take nothing). Shield 0: the train takes Damage (bleeds to fuel, then hull) and players within Radius of target take PlayerDamage. spareHull drops a hull hit (one multi-hit attack spends at most one). During a jump it does nothing (a glob still in the air when the jump starts). Returns the point used and whether the hit landed on the hull.
