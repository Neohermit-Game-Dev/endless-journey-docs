Sturmlanze

The tier 4 energy train weapon: a heavy laser turret that a player mans and fires by holding the trigger. It is a grid placeable (max 1 per train), crafted at the Workbench after the tier 4 TrainWep_Sturmlanze research (needs the Burst Cannon node). It runs on the same server core as the BurstCannon but has no magazine: it has its own heat bar instead. The longer the beam stays on, the wider and stronger it gets, until it overheats.

Packets: ReqMountSturmlanze, ReqSturmlanzeTrigger, ReqSturmlanzeAim (unreliable). There is no per-tick beam packet. See Networking Map.

Attributes on the placed Model: Firing, Heat (0..1, throttled), Overheated, AimYaw, AimPitch, GunnerUserId (0 = none), plus the HUD contract DisplayName, MaxHeat (1), PeakHeat.

How it works​

Heat. While the beam is on, heat rises at HeatPerSec (lowered by CannonHeatReduction); while it is off, heat falls at CoolPerSec. At 1 the cannon overheats: the beam cuts off and stays off until heat has cooled to 0, even if the trigger is still held. Releasing early keeps the heat.

Size and damage. power = clamp(Heat / PeakHeat, 0, 1). Width goes from MinWidth to MaxWidth and damage per second from MinDPS to MaxDPS. Best play (heat kept in the 75-99% band) averages about 200 DPS, above the CIWS (165) and the Burst Cannon's best play (150), below the tier 4 physical Schwarmfeuer (220).

Beam tick. Every 1 / TickRate seconds the server spherecasts from its own muzzle along the gunner's aim, clamped into AimConeDeg around the barrel. The cast stops at the first hit: an enemy takes the tick's damage (TrainWeaponDamage.applyDirect), a rock takes it through RockService.Damage, anything else just stops the beam. Rocks have no server parts, so RockService.SegmentHit runs on the same segment and the nearer hit wins. Damage is multiplied by 1 + CannonDamageBonus.

Unmanned fire. With no gunner and a Smart Cannon or Orbital Sentinel holder, the turret tracks the nearest visible enemy through the core's auto-aim and fires while the barrel is on target. Damage is multiplied by AutoLoaderRate, the holder gets the credit, and the cannon heats and overheats like a manned one.

Client rig and beam (all players)​

SturmlanzeController keeps one TurretRig per Model in workspace.PlacedObjects with PlaceableName = "Sturmlanze" (Discovery, one Janitor per turret). It finds PrimaryPart.Yaw and Turret_Laser_Body.Pitch (WaitForChild, 10 s timeout), snaps the rig to the current AimYaw / AimPitch (a late joiner sees no sweep from 0) and builds the turret's BeamFx. Each frame (BindToRenderStep("SturmlanzeRigs", Camera + 4), after the Gunner's step at Camera + 3) it eases the rig and then draws or hides the beam.

  • Who sees a beam: every client reads Firing from the Model each frame, so a late joiner or a streamed-in turret that is already firing shows the beam at the current width at once. The local gunner of that turret shows it from the trigger instead (trigger and seated and Overheated ~= true, the server's gates), so it starts on the press and stops on release or overheat without waiting for the attribute.
  • Origin and direction: the muzzle from BurstCannonMath.muzzle(jointFrame(Base.CFrame * Yaw.C0, Yaw.C1:Inverse() * Pitch.C0, rig.yaw, rig.pitch), MuzzleTip), the same math as the server. The local gunner's direction is its aim clamped into AimConeDeg around the barrel, like the server's.
  • Beam: BeamFx casts one cosmetic ray from the origin along the direction (ignoring the turret and the gunner's character) to the first part hit, else MaxRange. A cyan glow Part (SturmlanzeMath.width(heat) wide) and a brighter core Part (40% of it) span the ray; an impact glow sits at the end while the ray hits something. The core turns near white-cyan as power reaches 1, so the player can see the full-power threshold. The heat the width follows is eased on the client (10 per second), so the throttled Heat attribute does not step the width. The Parts are anchored neon, CanCollide, CanQuery and CanTouch off, exist only on the client, and are destroyed with the turret's Janitor. The end point may differ a little between clients; damage is only the server's.
  • Stops everywhere: when the gunner dies, leaves, or the turret is stashed, Gunner calls OnDismount (the predicted beam and the loop sound stop at once) and the server sets Firing to false, which hides the beam on every client.
  • Sounds: StartSound plays at the turret when Firing turns true and OverheatSound when Overheated turns true, on every client (SoundManager.PlaySFXAt). BeamSound loops for the local gunner only (SoundManager.PlayLooped is not positional) while the predicted beam is on, and fades out in 0.1 s. Sound ids in SoundConfig are blank until filled; PlayLooped then returns nil and the loop is skipped.

Gunner controls (local player)​

The controls are the shared TrainTurret/Gunner.luau (see BurstCannon). SturmlanzeController passes it a GunnerDef with the Sturmlanze config, the three packets, and two hooks: OnStep records the predicted-beam state and the loop sound, and OnDismount clears them. There is no CanTap (no tap latch: the beam needs the button held) and no OnKey (nothing to reload). The mount prompt sits on Cockpit.MountPromptPart.

The HUD is the generic TurretGui in heat mode (the model's MaxHeat attribute switches it on): one heat bar with teal / amber / rust bands, a peak glow and sweep from PeakHeat, and a blinking OVERHEATED state. The attribute contract and layout are in the Gunner HUD section of BurstCannon.

Data flow​

sequenceDiagram
    participant Cl as Gunner client
    participant Sv as SturmlanzeServer + TrainTurretServer
    participant All as All clients

    Cl->>Sv: ReqMountSturmlanze(model)
    Sv->>Sv: alive, PromptRange from Seat, single live gunner
    Sv->>Sv: GunnerUserId = player, Seat:Sit(humanoid)
    Sv-->>Cl: accepted

    loop AimReportHz
        Cl-)Sv: ReqSturmlanzeAim(yaw, pitch, dir) - unreliable
    end
    Sv->>All: AimYaw / AimPitch attributes

    Cl->>Sv: ReqSturmlanzeTrigger(true, dir)
    Note over Cl: predicted beam and loop sound start at once
    loop every Heartbeat while held and not Overheated
        Sv->>Sv: heat = stepHeat(...)
        Sv->>All: Firing = true, Heat (throttled) attributes
    end
    loop every 1/TickRate
        Sv->>Sv: spherecast from the server muzzle, dir clamped to aim cone
        Sv->>Sv: nearer of cast and RockService.SegmentHit: applyDirect / RockService.Damage
    end
    Note over All: each client draws the beam from its own rig muzzle
    alt heat reaches 1
        Sv->>All: Overheated = true, Firing = false
        Note over All: OverheatSound, beam off until heat is 0
    end
    Cl->>Sv: ReqSturmlanzeTrigger(false, dir)
    Sv->>All: Firing = false (heat cools at CoolPerSec)

Balance rule. The Sturmlanze is the tier 4 energy turret: stronger than every tier 3 turret, and below the tier 4 physical Schwarmfeuer (220 best play), as physical beats energy at each tier. The tier 3 turrets are the CIWS (physical, 165 DPS sustained) and the Burst Cannon (energy, 150 DPS best play). Keep

MaxDPS x CoolPerSec / (HeatPerSec + CoolPerSec) ~= 200, above both

At the defaults that is 333 x 0.25 / (1/6 + 0.25) = ~200 DPS for best play (a gunner who keeps heat in the 75-99% band, firing 60% of the time at full power). A gunner who holds the trigger to overheat and waits out the lock (6 s firing, 4 s locked) averages about 153 DPS, so the gap is the reward for managing heat. Only one fits on a train. If HeatPerSec, CoolPerSec, MaxDPS or a tier 3 turret's numbers change, re-check the formula (SturmlanzeMath.bestPlayDPS and its selfCheck, which asserts the whole ladder). CannonDamageBonus and AutoLoaderRate apply to both weapons, so they keep the ratio. CannonHeatReduction is the one exception, by design: it only slows heat gain, so best play becomes MaxDPS x CoolPerSec / (HeatPerSec x (1 - r) + CoolPerSec), about 228 DPS at r = 0.30 and 244 DPS at r = 0.45. That is the reward for buying the heat perks.

Damage follows beam-on time, not trigger presses: the server adds up the seconds the beam was on and pays dps x seconds every tick (and on the frame the beam stops), so pulsing or click spam cannot beat the duty-cycle figure above.
