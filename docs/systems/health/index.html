Health

The server is the single source of truth for every player's health value. HealthManager maintains an internal record, drives regen on RunService.Heartbeat, mirrors values to the Roblox Humanoid, and broadcasts to the owning client via HealthStateSync. The client forwards that packet to a signal consumed by the HUD.

How it works​

Server: authoritative record and regen heartbeat​

HealthManager is bootstrapped by HealthSystemLoader.server.luau via HealthManager.Initialize(). It maintains a HealthRecord per player (Health, LastDamageTime, LastSyncTime, LastSyncedHealth), created on PlayerAdded and deleted on PlayerRemoving — no stale data persists across sessions. CharacterAdded unconditionally resets record.Health to MaxHealth and mirrors it to humanoid.MaxHealth/humanoid.Health.

The regen heartbeat runs on RunService.Heartbeat, capped at MAX_HEALTH_STEP_DT = 0.1 seconds per step to prevent delta-time spikes from applying burst healing. While eligible (alive, not full, past RegenDelay since last damage), it adds HealthRegen * dt to the record each step. Syncs are batched: regen fires HealthStateSync at most every HEALTH_SYNC_INTERVAL = 0.1 seconds, or immediately when health reaches full.

Damage and armor mitigation​

HealthManager.ApplyDamage validates AttackType/AOEVariant, clamps the amount to [0, MaxHealth], then applies armor mitigation before subtracting from the record:

amount *= (1 - clamp(ArmorBaseReduction * (1 + ArmorEfficiency), 0, ArmorConfig.MaxReductionCap))
record.Health -= amount
record.LastDamageTime = os.clock()
humanoid.Health = record.Health
-- HealthStateSync:FireClient(player, record.Health)

applyDamageInternal reads ReplicatedStorage/ArmorState/<UserId>.ArmorBaseReduction (written by ArmorManager from the player's crafted armor tier) and PerkBuffs/<UserId>.ArmorEfficiency (the Armor perk) on every hit — crafting a tier or allocating/respeccing the perk takes effect on the next damage event, and with no armor the perk does nothing. See Perks for the cap rationale and the attacker-bonus-then-armor multiplication order. HealthManager.HealPlayer is the mirror path: record.Health = min(MaxHealth, record.Health + amount), mirrored and synced the same way.

CombatService is the only caller of ApplyDamage for weapon and NPC-collision damage — see Weapons → CombatService for the routing logic.

Client bridge​

HealthController.client.luau listens to Packets.HealthStateSync.OnClientEvent and re-fires Signals.OnHealthValueChanged with the received health value. It owns no state. HealthStaminaGuiController (not part of this system) consumes OnHealthValueChanged to animate the HUD bar.

Dev command​

/hurt (aliases /damage, /dmg) in the chat command bar damages the caller for 10% of their own max health, for exercising the HUD and the damage pipeline. It is registered client-side in NovaChat's Commands.client.luau and handled by DevCommandsService.server.luau, which gates it on place owner / group rank 250+ and ignores unauthorised callers. It routes through HealthManager.ApplyDamage, so armour reduction applies — the health actually lost is less than 10% whenever the player has armour.

Gotchas​

The HUD bar is the parent Frame, not a layer or the CanvasGroup

HealthStaminaGui authors each bar as three stacked art layers (healthDarker / healthLighter / healthWhite) inside a parent Health Frame, which in turn sits inside a clipping CanvasHealth CanvasGroup. The parent Frame is what carries the fill: HealthStaminaGuiController drives Health.Size = UDim2.fromScale(ratio, 1). Resizing one of the art layers distorts only that layer, and resizing the CanvasGroup moves the whole widget including its backing art. The stamina bar mirrors this exactly (CanvasStamina → Stamina → stamDarker / stamLighter / stamWhite). These names are load-bearing — renaming any of them in Studio breaks the controller at load.

Non-CombatService damage bypasses HealthManager

Any damage source that writes directly to Humanoid.Health — such as default Roblox fall damage or unrouted hazards — bypasses HealthManager's internal record, causing the HUD to show a stale value and skipping armor mitigation. Before shipping such a source, either disable it or listen to Humanoid.HealthChanged and reconcile the record.

One-way mirror: HealthManager writes record.Health → humanoid.Health but never reads back from the Humanoid. Any external write to humanoid.Health is invisible to the manager until a HealthStateSync event corrects the client. Keep all health mutations going through HealthManager.ApplyDamage or HealthManager.HealPlayer.

Character respawn resets health: CharacterAdded unconditionally resets record.Health to MaxHealth. If a partial-health respawn mechanic is ever needed, this reset must be conditional.

Sync rate: HealthStateSync is sent at most every 0.1 seconds during regen, but every ApplyDamage call forces an immediate sync — avoid calling it in tight per-frame loops.

Coverage is bounded by the chokepoint: damage that bypasses HealthManager.ApplyDamage also bypasses armor mitigation.

Data flow​

sequenceDiagram
    participant S as HealthManager (Server)
    participant H as Humanoid
    participant C as HealthController (Client)

    Note over S: PlayerAdded -> create HealthRecord{Health=MaxHealth}
    Note over S: CharacterAdded -> reset Health to MaxHealth
    S->>H: humanoid.MaxHealth / humanoid.Health = record.Health
    S->>C: HealthStateSync:FireClient(player, health)
    C->>C: Signals.OnHealthValueChanged:Fire(health)

    loop RunService.Heartbeat (server)
        alt regen eligible (alive, not full, past RegenDelay)
            S->>S: record.Health += HealthRegen * dt
            S->>H: humanoid.Health = record.Health
            opt periodic sync (0.1s) or full
                S->>C: HealthStateSync:FireClient
            end
        end
    end

    Note over S: HealthManager.ApplyDamage(amount, type, ...)
    S->>S: amount *= (1 - clamp(ArmorBaseReduction * (1+ArmorEfficiency), 0, MaxReductionCap))
    S->>S: record.Health -= amount; record.LastDamageTime = os.clock()
    S->>H: humanoid.Health = record.Health
    S->>C: HealthStateSync:FireClient

    Note over S: HealthManager.HealPlayer(amount)
    S->>S: record.Health = min(MaxHealth, record.Health + amount)
    S->>H: humanoid.Health = record.Health
    S->>C: HealthStateSync:FireClient
