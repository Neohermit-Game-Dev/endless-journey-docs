Rock Hazards

In the Ice, Volcano, Storm and Void biomes, rocks fly at the front of the train. Players shoot them down. A rock that gets through breaks on the shield dome in front of the train head, or — when the shield is 0 — flies through and hits the nose. Pressure rises over a biome stay: no rocks for the first minute, then a smooth ramp to a plateau at 12:00 that lasts until the Leviathan arrives.

How it works​

Motion​

A front rock moves in a straight line at constant speed: RockMath.positionAt(spawnPos, velocity, spawnTime, t) (eruption rocks add gravity, see Eruptions). The server sends those values once in RockSpawn; every client moves its own pooled copy with the same formula, so there is no per-frame network traffic. Braking does not slow rocks. The spin is cosmetic.

Spawning​

Every ROLL_INTERVAL the server rolls RockMath.spawnChance(t, …) when the train has started, no jump is active, the game is not over, workspace.CurrentBiome is in RockConfig.Biomes, and fewer than MAX_ACTIVE rocks are live. t is the time in the current biome; a CurrentBiome change resets it. A rock spawns at a random point in the SpawnGroup = "Rocks" volume (SpawnVolumes.Part3) and aims at a random point in the dome's rim plane, so every rock meets the dome. Model, size multiplier (SCALE_MIN..SCALE_MAX on the model's own scale) and spin axis are random; the biome sets material, color and transparency.

Storm front rocks use the Ice rates in a dark wet Slate look; Void front rocks use the Volcano rates (the last, hardest biome) in dark Basalt. RockConfig.BiomeOrder is { "Ice", "Volcano", "Storm", "Void" }: its index is on the wire, so new biomes are appended, never reordered.

Shooting​

Rocks have no server parts. Weapons test them with sphere math through RockService: hitscan at the lag-compensated time, rocket and cannon shells per flight step, and every player explosion through ApplyAOE. HP = HP_PER_SCALE × mult. See Weapons and Cannon.

Impact​

At spawn the server computes when the rock reaches the dome sphere (dome radius + rock radius). At that time:

  • shield above 0 → TrainShieldManager.ApplyDamage(DAMAGE_PER_SCALE × mult, "Rock", { Position = dome surface point, Severity }), the rock breaks on the dome (ShieldHit). Severity maps the size multiplier onto SHIELD_HIT_SEVERITY (0.3–0.6), so a bigger rock makes a bigger flash; damage never sets the visuals;
  • shield at 0 → the rock flies on to the nose plane, then calls ApplyDamage (with the nose point as Position), which bleeds to fuel and then the hull (HullHit). See Train Shield.

A jump or game over despawns every rock. Delayed callbacks check rock identity, so a removed rock never lands.

The dome​

ReplicatedStorage.ShieldDome (the dome template; the client clones it for the front and rear domes) is a half sphere of 8 Beams (2 rings × 4 quarter circles, the same beam as the ShieldTubeSegment tube) with a Radius attribute. Its PrimaryPart position and Radius are the server's collision sphere, so moving or resizing it in Studio moves the collision too. Rings, not slices: narrow slices repeat the texture and look striped. Its look (hidden by default, hit flash, reveals, break / reboot) belongs to the shield — see Train Shield — Dome. RockController does not read the shield: because the dome is hidden most of the time, a rock is not held at its surface (it would freeze in mid-air). It flies on for one network delay (~1.5–3 studs at SPEED 15) until RockRemoved arrives.

Eruptions​

In a biome with a RockConfig.Eruptions rule, a source appears from time to time in a band around the train and throws rocks at the whole train: front, middle and rear. This is a second rock kind (RockConfig.Kind.Eruption) in the same RockService registry, so weapons hit eruption rocks with no weapon changes. The front rocks above are unchanged and keep their own cap.

BiomeSourceRocks
VolcanoA fire pillar rises out of the haze (y −70)6–10 small lava rocks
IceA frozen-cloud chunk cracks high above (y 250)3–5 big ice shards that drift out and fall
StormA waterspout rises out of the sea fog (y −70)4–7 medium wreck debris pieces
VoidA mini rift (small black hole) opens beside the train at deck height (y 20)4–7 medium void rocks in floaty arcs

The rule is read per event: RockService looks up Eruptions[CurrentBiome] at each roll and uses that rule for the whole event.

Spawn band. The train line is the segment from the front dome center to the rear dome center (TrainPlatforms.RearX − TrainConfig.SHIELD_REAR_INSET), read at each event, so a longer train moves the band too. A source spawns at PILLAR_Y where the XZ distance to that line is between INNER and OUTER: a rounded box around the train (RockMath.eruptionPoint).

Timeline. The server fires EruptionStart(position, eruptTime) with eruptTime = now + WARNING: clients show the warning. At eruptTime, if no jump started, the game is not over and the current biome still maps to the same rule, the server spawns ROCKS_MIN–ROCKS_MAX rocks while under the rule's MAX_ACTIVE.

Motion. Each rock aims at a random deck point (X between the dome centers, Z within DECK_HALF_WIDTH of the line, Y = DECK_Y) with a flight time of FLIGHT_MIN–FLIGHT_MAX. RockMath.launchVelocity solves the launch velocity; positionAt adds ½·GRAVITY·dt². RockSpawn carries kind and biomeIndex, so clients find the same rule and add the same gravity. Ice uses a low gravity (−20): from 250 studs up, −60 would launch the shards upward first. Void uses −30: from deck height, −20 brings far rocks in low from the side, under the shield's upper half; −30 is the weakest gravity whose arcs meet it from every spot in the band.

Impact. The shield is the upper half of a capsule around the train line with the dome radius (RockMath.timeToHalfCapsule, rock radius added).

  • The arc meets it while the shield is above 0 → ApplyDamage(DAMAGE, DAMAGE_SOURCE, { Position = shell point, Severity = SEVERITY }), removed as ShieldHit.
  • Shield at 0 → the rock flies on to its deck point at the end of its flight → ApplyDamage(DAMAGE, DAMAGE_SOURCE, …) (bleeds to fuel, then hull). One event spends at most one hull hit: once a rock of that event lands on the hull, its other rocks pass spareHull = true (see Train Shield), so an event on an empty train counts like one front rock. Also, every living player within PLAYER_RADIUS of the impact takes PLAYER_DAMAGE as "AOE" / "Explosion" through HealthManager.ApplyDamage. Removed as HullHit (shatter burst).

Client. On EruptionStart, RockController takes the rule of CurrentBiome (eruptions never start during a jump, and the biome only changes inside one) and clones ReplicatedStorage.<PILLAR_TEMPLATE> at the position. Its enabled emitters and light are the warning. At eruptTime it bursts every emitter that has an EmitCount attribute, enables the rest, and plays the rule's SFX at the source. PILLAR_LIFETIME later every emitter, light and beam switches off, every visible part of the source (Transparency < 1; only the Void rift orb has any) shrinks to nothing over 1 s with MotionMaster, and Debris removes the source once the particles die. Eruption rocks use their rule's Material / Color / Transparency and gravity. Rock pools hold MAX_ACTIVE + the largest rule MAX_ACTIVE per model.

Studio setup​

  • ReplicatedStorage.RockAssets — rock Models (each with a PrimaryPart; parts anchored, CanCollide/CanTouch off). Any model works in any biome.
  • ReplicatedStorage.RockShatterVFX — a part with ParticleEmitters; an EmitCount attribute sets the burst size. The shield-hit burst is ReplicatedStorage.ShieldHitVFX, owned by Train Shield.
  • ReplicatedStorage.ShieldDome (name and attribute in TrainConfig.DOME_NAME / DOME_RADIUS_ATTRIBUTE; Radius attribute; authored at the front-dome spot — the server reads its PrimaryPart as the collision center), Workspace.RockVisuals (folder).
  • ReplicatedStorage.EruptionPillar (Volcano PILLAR_TEMPLATE) — a Model whose Base part (anchored, CanCollide/CanQuery/CanTouch off, invisible) holds Smoke (enabled), Fire (disabled), Embers (disabled, EmitCount 60) and a PointLight.
  • ReplicatedStorage.IceFall (Ice PILLAR_TEMPLATE) — same layout: Frost mist (enabled), Crack falling ice dust (disabled), Shards (disabled, EmitCount 30) and a dim cold PointLight.
  • ReplicatedStorage.Waterspout (Storm PILLAR_TEMPLATE) — Swirl mist (enabled), Column rising water (disabled), and Burst water spray (disabled, EmitCount 40) on a Top attachment 110 studs up, so it shows above the fog. No light.
  • ReplicatedStorage.VoidRift (Void PILLAR_TEMPLATE) — Base plus BlackHoleModel, a copy of the Art Place black hole scaled to a ~40-stud rim: dark Neon BlackOrb core, grey Neon GlowOrb rim, two beams (all parts anchored, CanCollide/CanQuery/CanTouch off). SpaceSmokeEmitter ×3 and DistortionEmitter (the dark red twirl, spinning 360°/s) are enabled; FXPartSmoke.SmokeEmitter turns on at the eruption; Teleport1/2/3 sparks are bursts (EmitCount 60 / 30 / 15). GlowOrb.Twirl (a disabled BillboardGui) is for the far rift scenery only, see Biomes — Void. No light.
  • A missing template → one warning per name; rocks still fly. The Eruption, IceFall, Waterspout and VoidRift SFX in SoundConfig are silent until ids are set.
  • A SpawnVolumes part with SpawnGroup = "Rocks". Junk disables this group. Part3 sits ~50 studs from the nose for testing; move it ~250–400 studs ahead for play.

Tuning​

Config module: RockConfig. Key fields: Biomes[b] (Material, Color, Transparency, P_START, P_PEAK), SCALE_MIN/SCALE_MAX, SPEED, SPIN_SPEED, AIM_SPREAD, HP_PER_SCALE, DAMAGE_PER_SCALE, SHIELD_HIT_SEVERITY, ROLL_INTERVAL, GRACE, PEAK_AT, MAX_ACTIVE; eruptions in Eruptions[b] (see Eruptions). See Config Map.

Not in scope​

Late joiners do not see rocks already in flight; no reward for a destroyed rock; Shield Nova and enemy explosions do not affect rocks.
