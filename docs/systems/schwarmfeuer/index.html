Schwarmfeuer

A train-mounted twin missile-pod turret that a player mans. It is the tier 4 turret of the physical line: a grid placeable (max 1 per train), crafted at the Workbench after the TrainWep_Schwarmfeuer research (needs TrainWep_CIWS). The gunner holds the trigger and sweeps the crosshair over enemies to lock them, then releases to fire every loaded missile (12 when full) as one salvo. The salvo is split over the locks (smart divide), the missiles home in, and each one bursts with a medium splash. Then the pods reload from the party's train ammo pool (Missile, crafted as CraftMissiles x12, hidden until TrainWep_Schwarmfeuer is done).

Mount, seating, aim, turn rate, unmanned Smart Cannon / Orbital Sentinel fire, Stash / Move prompts and the generic TurretGui (magazine mode) are the shared train-turret code, as on the CIWS. This page lists what is new.

How a salvo works​

sequenceDiagram
    participant G as Gunner client
    participant S as SchwarmfeuerServer
    participant A as All clients
    G->>S: ReqSchwarmfeuerTrigger(true, dir)
    S-->>A: PodsOpen = true (doors swing open)
    Note over G: hold: enemies in the lock cone for LockTime get a lock + diamond
    G->>S: ReqSchwarmfeuerTrigger(false, dir)
    G->>S: ReqSchwarmfeuerFire(locks)
    Note over S: validLocks, then salvoTargets(locks, 12)
    loop 12 missiles, every LaunchInterval
        S-->>A: SchwarmfeuerLaunched(turret, tube, origin, dir, target, locks)
        Note over A: Ammo -1 hides that tube's tip; a visual missile flies
    end
    S-->>A: Reloading = true, PodsOpen = false
    Note over S: each missile bursts: applyDirect + applyBlast
    S-->>A: ExplosionAt(center, 6, "Schwarmfeuer")
  • Locking (client). While the trigger is held, the pods are full and the gunner is seated, every live enemy (EnemyNPC / EnemyShip tags) inside LockConeDeg (4°) of the crosshair, within MaxRange of the pitch pivot and in sight of the camera builds up dwell time; after LockTime (0.3 s) it is locked. One lock per enemy, at most 12. A lock drops when its enemy dies or leaves range. Leaving the cone before the lock restarts the dwell.
  • Fire (server). On release the client sends its locks once. The server accepts the salvo only from a seated gunner with loaded pods (Ammo > 0) and no salvo in flight (canFire). validLocks keeps live enemies, once each, within MaxRange and within ServerLockConeDeg (60°) of the barrel, in order, at most 12. "Live enemies" is the set of Models TrainTurretServer.liveEnemies() lists, built once per packet. No valid lock (a quick tap) fires the salvo unguided along the barrel.
  • Smart divide. salvoTargets gives the 12 missiles their targets round-robin over the locks in lock order: 1 lock gets all 12; 5 locks get 3, 3, 2, 2, 2.
  • Launch. The first missile waits until the doors have finished opening (firstLaunchAt: DoorOpenTime after they started), then one missile every LaunchInterval (0.08 s) from tube 13 - Ammo (MissileTips, pitch-joint frame), so the order alternates pods. A started salvo finishes after the gunner leaves. The last missile closes the pods.
  • Train ammo. A placed turret starts empty. Whenever it is empty and the pool has missiles, it reloads on its own; the missiles are taken when the reload ends, so a short pool gives a partial magazine, and a partial magazine fires a partial salvo (canFire and the client lock gate need Ammo > 0, the queue is sized to Ammo). Stash / Move gives the loaded missiles back.
  • Flight. stepMissile: straight for BoostTime (0.15 s), then turn toward the target by at most TurnRateDeg (240°/s) at MissileSpeed (160 studs/s). Near the target the limit rises so the turn radius is never more than half the distance to it, so close or off-axis targets are hit instead of orbited (the self-check asserts six such targets are hit within 1.5 s). A missile whose target died takes the nearest live lock of its salvo (retarget), else flies straight.
  • Burst. The first of: a rock on the step (RockService.SegmentHit), any live enemy within FuseRadius (2) of the step, a world hit (raycast without the turret and the shooter), or MaxFlightTime (5 s, air burst). Full Damage to the hit enemy (or the rock), then applyBlast with BlastRadius (6) on the rest, then ExplosionAt. Perk CannonDamageBonus scales Damage.
  • Unmanned. With a Smart Cannon / Orbital Sentinel holder and no gunner, once the core's auto-aim is on target the turret locks that target plus the nearest other enemies in range (up to 12) and fires a salvo credited to the holder. It then waits autoCycleDelay, so unmanned fire runs at AutoLoaderRate × the manned cycle.
  • Pods. Trigger-down opens them; a release does not close them at once, because the Fire packet arrives a frame later. Open pods with no salvo queued close once the trigger has been up for PodCloseGrace (0.3 s) (podsIdle), which also covers a gunner who leaves or a Fire the server rejects. A trigger held through the end of a reload reopens them.
  • Turret removed. Stashing or moving the turret drops its missiles in flight (its Janitor).

Client​

SchwarmfeuerController keeps one entry per placed turret (Discovery, one Janitor each):

  • Rig. A TurretRig on Base.Yaw / Body.Frame.Pitch, eased to the replicated aim, at Camera + 4 after the Gunner's step.
  • Doors. The 12 Gun.DoorN.Hinge joints follow PodsOpen over DoorOpenTime (0.25 s, smoothstep): Hinge.Transform = CFrame.Angles(0, OpenAngle × open, 0). Each row opens as a pair, outward and forward, clear of the flight paths.
  • Tips. Tube k shows while k > MagazineSize - Ammo (LocalTransparencyModifier), so every client hides the tip of each launched missile and shows all 12 again when the reload refills Ammo.
  • Missile visuals. Each SchwarmfeuerLaunched clones that tube's missile (welds stripped, anchored), adds a grey smoke Trail and steps it with the same stepMissile toward the target's current position. It ends at the nearest ExplosionAt within BlastRadius + 4, at the target's fuse radius, or at MaxFlightTime. The visual is cosmetic; damage is only the server's.
  • Sounds. SchwarmfeuerLock (gunner, per lock), SchwarmfeuerLaunch (per missile), SchwarmfeuerDoor (each PodsOpen edge), SchwarmfeuerReload (Reloading → true), SchwarmfeuerExplode (per burst; WeaponFx has no entry for turrets).

Rig (template)​

Built in Studio (v3), ~12 × 14.6 × 13.5 studs, 85 parts; the model pivot (Base.PivotOffset) is upright at the floor under the yaw axis.

  • Base (anchored PrimaryPart, union: low bolted pedestal) → Base.Yaw Motor6D (1.4 studs above the floor, world-aligned) → Body.Frame.
  • Body.Frame (union): turntable, octagon pylon trunk (top at 7.4 studs above the floor so the head clears it at +45°), monitor boom + yoke, rear seat outrigger with armrests and footrest. Body.Seat, Body.MountPromptPart, side sticks, Body.Monitor hung between the yoke pivots. Frame.Pitch Motor6D (axle 7.28 studs above the floor) → Gun.GunBody.
  • Gun.GunBody (union, pitches): axle drum with bearings, octagon sensor head (optic window with three lenses, brow lip, side vents, phased-array radar on a mast), side shafts, two octagon pods (front recess with door-frame grid and hinge knuckles, 12 bores, top plate, cheek, rear exhaust slots).
  • Gun.Missile1..12: red tip + shaft + band, Launch attachment at the tip; odd = left pod, even = right pod (launch order).
  • Gun.Door1..12 (one union each: bevelled leaf, raised boss, two knuckles, latch), each with a Hinge Motor6D (Part0 GunBody) on its outer edge and attribute OpenAngle (±120).
  • CollisionBox (anchored, no collide) over the footprint.

Tuning​

Config module: SchwarmfeuerConfig.

FieldDefaultEffect
MagazineSize / ReloadTime12 / 5 sOne salvo per magazine
AmmoType"Missile"Train ammo pool type
LockTime / LockConeDeg / ServerLockConeDeg0.3 s / 4 / 60Lock dwell, client crosshair cone, server sanity cone
LaunchInterval0.08 s12 missiles in ~0.9 s
Damage / BlastRadius / FuseRadius114 / 6 / 2Per missile: full to the hit enemy, then the splash
MissileSpeed / BoostTime / TurnRateDeg160 / 0.15 s / 240Flight and homing
MaxRange / MaxFlightTime600 / 5 sLock range; air burst
DoorOpenTime / PodCloseGrace0.25 s / 0.3 sDoor swing (the first missile waits for it); idle pods close after the release
Aim / mountas the CIWSPitchMax 45, PitchMin -15, MaxPlaced 1

Balance. Best play on one target = MagazineSize × Damage / (LockTime + (MagazineSize − 1) × LaunchInterval + ReloadTime) = 12 × 114 / 6.18 ≈ 221 DPS: above the tier 4 energy Sturmlanze (200), as physical beats energy at each tier. The splash adds on top against groups. SchwarmfeuerMath.selfCheck asserts 216–224; SturmlanzeMath.selfCheck asserts the Schwarmfeuer stays above the Sturmlanze and below 1.15 × it.

Recipe: CraftSchwarmfeuer (Tier 4, Metal 160 + Wood 40, TrainWep_Schwarmfeuer). Research node TrainWep_Schwarmfeuer: tier 4, column 0, needs TrainWep_CIWS, Metal 200 + Wood 40, 180 s. Sound ids are blank in SoundConfig.
