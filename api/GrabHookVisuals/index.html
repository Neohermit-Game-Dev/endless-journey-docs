GrabHookVisuals

Client-side VFX for the grabhook tool. Every pull is one rig cloned from ReplicatedStorage.GrabHookVFX:

  • HookHead — gunmetal + neon head (pivot at the rear, LookVector = flight direction) with a TetherEnd attachment and a short Trail.
  • Tether — a thin Neon cylinder core (refit every frame so the tether looks round from every angle) carrying the Glow and Energy beams.
  • HitFX — attachment with the latch LatchRing + Burst emitters, hold Sparks and a PointLight.

Phases: flight (head travels from the handle, trail on, slack rope), hold (head embedded TipDepth inside the item, latch hit, rope pulls tight, item reels to the handle), release (sparks stop, tether and light fade over ReleaseFadeTime, everything is destroyed).

One RenderStepped connection drives every active rig. Each rig owns one Janitor. Tuning: GrabHookConfig.Effects and GrabHookConfig.Physics.

Reel speed in studs/s (perks applied); defaults to Physics.RetractSpeed.

) → ()

Plays the full grabhook VFX for the player who fired: flight from the handle, embed + latch hit, reel-in at retractSpeed, then fade out.

onCleanup runs once when the reel-in completes, or early if targetPart or startAtt leaves workspace (pool reclaim, unequip, death).
