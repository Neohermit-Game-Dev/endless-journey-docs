Pirate Ship

The pirate ship is the game's boss encounter. It sails in from far behind the train on the right, matches the train's speed beside the train, and fights until one side loses. It attacks with the two cannons on the side that faces the train and with pirates that leap onto the train. At 75 %, 50 % and 25 % health it switches sides: it loops out away from the train, comes back behind it, circles the train twice with a cannon barrage, and settles on the other side. The fight ends when the ship sinks (coins + EXP for everyone and tier 3+ junk), when the train falls (the normal game-over paths), or when the party warp-jumps away.

The train never moves (the world scrolls past it), so the ship "matching speed" means it comes to rest beside the train. World axes: the train faces −X, right = −Z.

How it works​

When it happens​

The Encounter channel rolls PirateAttack (weight 60) against Nothing (40) every RollInterval studs of travel, with a long PostEventDelay after each attack. PirateAttackEvent.CanTrigger uses the void swarm gate (VoidSwarmEvent.gateOpen): the crew has researched a ranged or train weapon, or the train has jumped once, and no jump is running. Only one ship exists at a time. A void swarm can run at the same time (its own channel).

The sequence​

sequenceDiagram
    participant O as EventOrchestrator
    participant E as PirateAttackEvent
    participant S as PirateShipService
    participant C as Clients
    O->>E: Start(ctx, finish)
    E->>C: Combat music (4 s crossfade), Right junk lane off
    E->>S: Spawn(onKilled, onSunk, onSide)
    S->>C: body far back-right, Phase = Approach (no damage)
    Note over S: lane-change arcs, then straight into the right hold
    S->>C: Phase = Fight, Health/MaxHealth set (boss bar shows)
    loop fight
        S->>C: PirateShipFx CannonFire / Impact, boarder leaps
        S->>C: HP crosses 75/50/25 % → Phase = Switch, Turn ±1
        Note over S: loop out, 2 barrage laps, settle
        S->>E: onSide(side) (lanes swap)
        S->>C: Phase = Fight, Side = other side
    end
    S->>E: onKilled(side) (lane on, SpawnBonusJunk on that side)
    S->>C: Phase = Sinking, Fx Sink
    S->>E: onSunk → finish()

  1. Start. PirateAttackActive = true, the Right SpawnHelper group is disabled (junk already out there drifts away; onSide moves the off lane to the ship's new side after each switch), and every player (and any joiner) gets the Combat music context.

  2. Approach. The body spawns SpawnRight studs right of the right hold point and far enough behind it for the path, nose forward. PirateShipPath.approach() turns in toward the train (ApproachTurn on an ApproachRadius arc), runs the diagonal, then curves right on an ApproachSettleRadius arc that ends exactly at the hold, at ApproachSpeed. DamageTakenMult = 0: nothing hurts it yet.

  3. In position. At Hold (the imported model's position, right of the train) it reads the player count, sets health (BaseHealth × (1 + PerExtraPlayer × (players − 1))), sets DamageTakenMult = 1, Phase = Fight, and starts the attack timers.

  4. Fight. It stays at its hold point (idle animation), fires its facing cannons and sends boarders.

  5. Side switch. When the health ratio crosses an unused SwitchAt threshold while in Fight, Phase = Switch and it follows PirateShipPath.switch(side): Every turn is away from the train: clockwise when it starts on the right, counter-clockwise from the left. It leans into every turn.

    1. Straight Forward, then a D-loop out: a LoopRadius half circle away from the train, straight back down past the train's rear, and a LoopRadius quarter turn in behind the train.
    2. Straight across behind the train to the middle of the rear.
    3. Laps full ovals around the train from there (radius = hold distance, straights = 2 × TrainHalfLength). The barrage fires every BarrageEvery, here only.
    4. A quarter oval up the other side, then straight into its hold point (Hold with Z mirrored across TrainCenter.Z).

    Speed ramps up and down over Ramp seconds (SwitchSpeed; about 69 s per switch). No boarders during a switch. It can always be damaged. On arrival every threshold the health has passed is used up (consumeThresholds), so a threshold crossed during a switch is skipped, and one burst across two thresholds gives one switch. The cannon and boarder timers restart.

Attacks​

  • Cannons (every CannonEvery in Fight, every BarrageEvery during the switch laps). Only the two cannons on the side that faces the train fire, alternating (facingCannons): left side in → cannons 4 + 2, right side in → cannons 3 + 1. Shots leave from the body's Muzzle1..4 attachments. Target: a living player on the train AimChance of the time, else a deck point (VoidEnemyService.PickTarget). The end point is clipped to the shield while it is up (EndPoint). Flight time = distance / BallSpeed; when the ship dies, nothing fires any more: balls still in flight vanish (no impact on the server, the client removes them and stops the fire animations). The hit is VoidEnemyService.Impact with CannonAttack (shield Severity 0.1): shield first, then the train (fuel, hull) and an explosion on nearby players.
  • Boarders (first at BoardFirst, then every BoardEvery, only in Fight). floor(BoardBase × player scale) pirates rolled from BoardComposition (SMG 35, Shotgunner 25, Pistoleer 20, Grenadier 12, Sniper 8) leap from the Deck attachment to random deck points in boardArea(side) (BoardArea on the right, mirrored on the left) with EnemySpawner.SpawnLeap, then the normal EnemyBrain AI takes over. They belong to the ship's wave: they die with the ship and vanish on a jump.

Damage and rewards​

The body is a Humanoid model tagged EnemyNPC + EnemyShip with AoeAtSurface = true and KillReward = PirateShipConfig.KillReward (see Enemy Combat). So every player weapon damages it through CombatService: lifesteal, Weak Spot and Finishing Touch apply. Each player hit fires PlayerDamagedShip (the Raid Defender quest), and the Health/MaxHealth attributes feed Wreck Salvage. If a player leaves mid-fight, max and current health drop by the same share (shrink); joiners change nothing. The MaxHealth attribute is written first, and Wreck Salvage (SalvagePerkMath.shipRolls) rolls nothing on a MaxHealth change, so a rescale never pays salvage.

The ship is not EnemyChrono-tracked: LagCompensation tests a tracked model only within 10 studs of its root, which would drop most hits on a 140-stud hull, so it is a plain server target. Hits on the moving ship (side switch) use the server pose; the client look follows tightly (LookEase).

The kill pays every living player KILL_XP × KillReward and KILL_COINS × KillReward, with bounty perks applied once on top, and fires kill perks once (it is one pirate kill). PirateAttackEvent then re-enables the ship's lane and fires SpawnBonusJunk(rollLoot, side) with the side of the ship's position at death: LootTiers (mostly tier 3, some tier 4) plus a tier 5 at LootRareChance, drifting past on the ship's side.

How it ends​

EndWhat happens
Ship health 0Sinking (also mid-switch: it stops where it is): boarders removed, Sink visual for SinkSeconds, then the body is destroyed and the event finishes. Rewards as above.
Train fallsOnTrainGameOver freezes the ship (Phase = Frozen, no attacks, no movement). The run's reset finishes the event and removes it.
Warp jump / resetThere is no time limit. On a jump the orchestrator force-finishes: ship and boarders removed at once (the jump transition hides it). No reward.

Every end path: PirateAttackActive = false, both lanes on, Normal music for everyone, ship gone (one Janitor per run).

Client​

PirateShipController clones the look for every ActiveEnemies child with a ShipLook attribute into workspace.PirateShipLooks, pivoted at root × LookOffset (the model's own LookVector is +X while its nose points −X). While the body publishes a move, the look samples the same PirateShipPath at GetServerTimeNow() − MoveStart every frame (and takes the lean turn from it). Following the replicated root instead stepped the whole ship: the anchored root's CFrame arrives at the network rate, not every frame. At rest it eases toward the root (1 - exp(-LookEase·dt)). The script only moves the look; every pose comes from the animation tracks. Animations on the look's AnimationController.Animator (PirateShipConfig.Anim):

TrackPriorityWhen
Idle / LeanLeft / LeanRightIdle, loopedAll three start in the same frame (in phase) and always play on ONE priority; weights idle = 1 − left − right, with left / right eased by smoothstep over LeanBlend from the turn. Roblox shows the weighted average of same-priority tracks, so this is a true crossfade. The old setup (leans alone on Movement at ~0 weight) averaged the two leans only with each other: 0.01 vs 0.001 already showed a 91 % lean, so every turn snapped the hull and wings
Fire[i]ActionCannon i fires
DeathAction4Sink fx; pinned on its last frame (a finished non-looped track lets go of the pose and the hull swung back up 38°)

Boss bar​

PirateBossBarController watches ActiveEnemies for the ShipLook body and enables StarterGui.PirateBossBar (slides in from the top) once Phase leaves Approach; it hides when the body leaves. Everything comes from body attributes, so late joiners see it too.

  • Frame: health bar (Health / MaxHealth, MotionMaster 0.2 s; rust, red under 25 %), 9 notches, health number (8,420 / 12,000; phones: current only). No ship name.
  • Strip (hidden in Approach, Fight and Frozen): Switch "BROADSIDE BARRAGE" (phones "BARRAGE!", Warning icon, amber) for the whole side switch; Sinking "SHIP DESTROYED" (phones "DESTROYED", Bolt icon, teal).
  • Layouts: wide (authored 1920×1022, UIScale = min(W/1920, H/1022), top center 12 px under the topbar) and compact when that scale is under 0.68 (authored 844×332, UIScale clamp 0.75–1.5): a 340×30 capsule centered in the topbar row (GuiService.TopbarInset) and a small auto-width chip with short texts. Under the jump vote (PartyVoteGui.Root) or the crew-down banner (CrewDownGui.CountdownBanner) while they show.
  • Phones: while the compact bar shows, the controller sets HiddenByBossBar on QuestTrackerGui; QuestTrackerController hides its panel while it is set.
  • Icons: PirateShipConfig.BossBarIcons (Warning, Bolt): white PNGs (sources in roblox-gui-prototypes/assets/pirate-boss-bar/), tinted per state by the controller.

Sounds: PirateCannonFire, PirateCannonImpact, PirateShipExplode in SoundConfig (ids empty until provided).

Studio setup​

The look is the user's imported model (kept with its animation saves as ServerStorage.PirateShip_AnimSource in Game Instance), cloned into EnemyModels.PirateShip with InitialPoses/AnimSaves removed, RootPart anchored (the animations drive the skinned-mesh bones under RootPart) and collide/query/touch off. Its pivot sits at Hold (the right idle point). The body's Muzzle1..4 attachments sit at the Cannon{i}_Barrel_Dark positions and Deck on the deck top, in root space where the root CFrame = PirateShipPath.hold("Right"). The three hit boxes (Hull = hull + deck, Wings = membrane, Rigging = sails) are the bounding boxes of those mesh groups. Cannons: 1 back-right, 2 back-left, 3 front-right, 4 front-left.

Gotchas​

  • PirateShipPath.selfCheck() walks every path (no jumps, nose along the path, gap to the train, full laps, every turn away from the train). PirateShipService.selfCheck() runs real ships in edit mode with stubbed collaborators (approach, idle fire, switch, skip, leaver, death mid-lap, game over): Ship:Step(now) is the whole server loop, Heartbeat only calls it. Load modules fresh with a loadstring require shim (the Command Bar caches require).
  • Visual preview in edit mode: paste tools/studio-previews/pirate-ship-preview.luau into the Command Bar (approach, fight, one switch, fight, sink on the real paths; the camera stays free).
  • The ship uses VoidEnemyService.Impact, so its hits obey the shield rule and do nothing during a jump.
  • /pirate (dev, owner / rank 250+) starts an attack outside the orchestrator; it ends on a jump like a normal run.

Tuning​

Everything is in PirateShipConfig (values are a first draft): position (Hold, LookOffset, TrainCenter, TrainHalfLength, SpawnRight, ApproachTurn, ApproachRadius, ApproachSettleRadius, ApproachSpeed), side switch (SwitchAt, Forward, LoopRadius, Laps, SwitchSpeed, Ramp, MinGap, BarrageEvery), health (BaseHealth, PerExtraPlayer, KillReward, SinkSeconds, SinkDepth), look (LookEase, LeanBlend), cannons (FirstCannon, CannonEvery, AimChance, BallSpeed, CannonAttack), boarders (BoardFirst, BoardEvery, BoardBase, BoardPerExtra, BoardComposition, BoardArea, LeapSeconds), loot (LootTiers, LootRareChance). Roll cadence is EventConfig.Channels.Encounter. Music is SoundConfig.Music.Playlists.Combat (per-biome tracks, 4 s fades).
