Void Enemies

Five flying void creatures attack the train: Void Cyst, Bileplume Drifter, Shatterbeak, Voltmaw and Sirenspine Chorus. The server owns invisible bodies with a Humanoid (so guns, the cannon, kill credit and perks work as on any NPC) and moves only their root part. Each client clones the full part-built look from a saved Studio model and animates it. Every enemy hit goes through one shield rule: while the train shield is above 0, it takes every attack, including attacks aimed at players.

How it works​

Bodies and looks​

A body (ServerStorage.EnemyAssets.<Id>, ModelStreamingMode = Atomic so a streaming client gets the root and the look together) is an invisible anchored HumanoidRootPart, a Humanoid (BreakJointsOnDeath and RequiresNeck off, display off) and 1–2 invisible hitboxes welded to the root (CanQuery on, so hitscan and AOE find them). VoidEnemyService.Spawn clones it, sets HP from config, tags it EnemyNPC, sets attribute Visual = <Id>, parents it to workspace.ActiveEnemies, and calls EnemyChrono.Track (player hitscan gets lag compensation).

A look (ReplicatedStorage.EnemyModels.<Id>) is the full part model. VoidEnemyController clones it for every body with a Visual attribute, eases it toward the body root (1 - exp(-LookEase·dt)) and poses it every frame within LookNear, every 3rd frame within LookFar, else every 10th. A missing look warns once per Id; the body still works.

An animated look has an AnimationController with an Animator (today: Shatterbeak, Voltmaw and Sirenspine, skinned meshes with clips Archetypes[id].Anims). The client loads one track per clip, skips the rig, and only pivots the look to the eased body CFrame on the same LOD cadence. One clip rule: body attribute Anim names only a base clip (Archetypes[id].Base: clip → priority; Voltmaw Idle / Swim, Sirenspine and Shatterbeak Move); the client crossfades the base tracks to it (0.15 s). Every attack clip is started by its VoidEnemyFx packet and chained on the client by VoidEnemyClips (Action priority, over the base clip; Death is Action4; looped attack clips in Archetypes[id].Loops): Shatterbeak DiveWindup → AttackStart, AttackLoop (held until Ram, capped at WindupTime + AttackMaxTime + 1 s); Ram → AttackEnd, Idle (IdleTime). Sirenspine SirenShockwave → Shockwave. Voltmaw below. A finished one-shot part holds its last frame until the next part, so Start → Loop never pops. With MoveClipBase (Sirenspine) and no PulseT0, the Move track runs at PatrolSpeed / MoveClipBase. PulseT0 (server time of a pulse start) and PulseRate (pulse speed, ReturnPulseRate while a squid swims back) set the Move track's speed to PulseRate and its time to PushTime + ((now − PulseT0 − PulseLag) × PulseRate) mod MoveLen, so the arm squeeze matches the surge the player sees (the look trails the body by about PulseLag s). Squids carry their up vector with the nose, so steep dives do not spin them. A Death clip (Shatterbeak, Voltmaw, Sirenspine) plays on Fx.Death: the look stays where it died, holds the clip's last frame, falls (DeathPlungeGravity) from the clip's DeathPlungeStart marker (a clip without one: after DeathPlungeDelay s, the clip length) for DeathPlungeTime s, and outlives the body (DeathLinger). The Sirenspine sinks (gravity 40, ~125 studs) belly-up and fades out over the last DeathFadeTime s of the sink.

Only the body root replicates its movement. The server sets it every Heartbeat along the current MoveTo; behaviors decide at 10 Hz.

Look rig format​

Editing a look in Studio needs no code as long as it keeps this format:

  • Root — invisible PrimaryPart at the model pivot; the name tag hangs on it.
  • Joints/J_<name> — invisible pivot parts. Attributes: Parent (another joint's name, or empty = the root), Kind, and the numbers that kind reads.
  • Every other part — attribute Joint = <name> follows that joint; no attribute = static.
  • A Parent loop (A → B → A) warns once and hangs the joint where the loop closes off the root.

Each frame: pose(joint) = pose(parent) * restOffset(joint) * wiggle(Kind, t). Rest offsets come from the saved model, so moving, resizing, recoloring, adding or deleting parts and joints just works. A new ray is a copy of an old ray's joints and parts.

Parts the client reads by name: Void Cyst Core and Tip, Drifter Nozzle. Keep these names. The Shatterbeak look is a skinned mesh (no rig parts): its pivot is the Root bone, nose at −Z, beak tip NoseLen (7.5) studs ahead of the pivot. The Sirenspine look is the imported Riftjaw skinned mesh (23 × 25 × 79 studs, never rescale it): pivot RootPart (front −Z), body centre 9.6 studs behind the pivot; the buff tethers start at the bones named in TetherBones (Crest1_Tip, Crest2_Tip, Barbel1_Tip, Barbel2_Tip). Its server body has 3 swim-pose hitboxes that cover the measured mesh plus ~2.5 studs (lax aim, good for mobile): Hitbox1 head 17 × 21 × 24.5 at z −20.25, Hitbox2 mid body 27.4 × 29.6 × 26 at z +5, Hitbox3 rear + tail 12 × 23 × 33 at z +34.5. Two more, HitboxCurlSide (46 × 20 × 42 at (25, −2, 31)) and HitboxCurlLift (10 × 18 × 26 at (0, 15, 39)), cover the tail while the Shockwave clip sweeps it out and lifts it: the behavior sets their CanQuery on only inside CurlBoxes windows (s after the clip start).

The Voltmaw look is the imported skinned fish (40.4 × 18.9 × 18.9 studs, never rescale it): pivot RootPart (front −Z), mesh top 10.8 studs above the pivot. Bolts and the beam start at the EyeMuzzle bone (the forehead crystal, (0, 3.81, −17.08) at rest = MuzzleOffset; aim = its UpVector); the client reads TransformedWorldCFrame every frame, and with no look (far, hidden) it uses the body root × MuzzleOffset, so every hit still gets its visual. Its server body has 3 hitboxes (mesh + 2.5 studs): Hitbox1 head 16 × 17.1 × 20.1 at (0, 0.4, −11.7), Hitbox2 body + sail + fins 16.4 × 20.8 × 18.3 at (0, 2.6, 7.5), Hitbox3 tail 7 × 12 × 7 at (0, 0, 20.2).

The shield rule​

VoidEnemyService.Impact(origin, target, attack, attacker) is the only way an enemy deals damage:

jump:        nothing (a glob still in the air when the jump starts)
shield > 0:  point = VoidEnemyMath.clipToShield(origin, target, front, rear, radius) or target
             TrainShieldManager.ApplyDamage(Damage, Source, { Position = point, Severity })
             -- players and deck take nothing, even when the attack was aimed at a player
shield == 0: TrainShieldManager.ApplyDamage(Damage, Source, nil, spareHull)  -- fuel, then hull
             CombatService.ApplyAOE(target, Radius, PlayerDamage, "AOE", attacker, { attacker })

The shield shape is the upper half-capsule around the front and rear dome centers (VoidEnemyMath.shieldLine, same shape RockService uses). Stepped paths (bile arc, a Shatterbeak flight) test each short step. EndPoint(origin, target) gives the same point without damage, so rushes and Shatterbeak flights aim at the shield surface. The client clips its visuals with the same math while TrainStateCache.ShieldFraction() > 0.

Every shield hit shows the forcefield for a random 0.5–1.25 s — see Train Shield — Dome.

The target is a player or deck point only when the line to it is within MaxAimOff degrees of square to the train, else the deck under the squid: the ram meets the shield near head-on, never grazing along it. Impact (Ram, the beak tip on the hit point), AttackEnd (recoil; on the shield the squid glances off, its velocity mirrored on the shield surface), Idle, Move back to the school (Move clip at ReturnPulseRate, velocity change capped at ReturnAccel, ClearMin + 10 studs from the center line)One Impact when the flight meets the shield, reaches the aim point (it never goes into the deck), or hits the AttackMaxTime cap (that ends the flight, not the squid)

Sirenspine1 + escort (2 Drifters, 4 Shatterbeaks)Spawns SpawnAhead studs ahead and swims in (ApproachSpeed easing to PatrolSpeed) on a line that meets its patrol circle on the tangent (no turn at the join). Then it swims a slow circle (PatrolRadius 80, more than its 79-stud body, so no tight turn) around a post PostAhead ahead of the nose, with a slow vertical wave (WaveAmp, WaveRate), facing its velocity and banking into the turn (BankK, at most BankMax°). The circle is PostLift above the nose, raised so its lowest point clears the shield dome by ShieldClear (VoidEnemyService.ShieldLine). Every PulseEvery it sends SirenShockwave (the client plays Shockwave; half speed, ShockwaveSlow)At BurstTime into the clip: the buff (below), the shockwave VFX (Pulse, below) and one Impact from its pivot at a player or deck point (PlayerAimChance)

All cooldowns divide by the enemy's AttackRateMult attribute (VoidEnemyService.Rate); a Shatterbeak school uses the highest value of its live squids; for the Voltmaw, the attack timer (counted at dt × AttackRateMult) and the charge (BeamStart is sped up to fit; beam, burst and clip parts keep their length).

PickTarget aims only at players who stand on the train (OnDeck: over the TrainGeometry.box (the built train, same height band around the deck as the old platform box), at most 15 studs above it), never at a player who fell off.

Sirenspine Shockwave VFX​

The template is ReplicatedStorage.SirenspineVfx (made in the Art Place, copied in unchanged): Burst (Attachment at the body centre, (0, 0, 9.6) in RootPart space; emitters ChargeGlow, ChargeCrackle, ShockSphere, BurstBolts), Arc (Beams ArcGlow, ArcCore), Impact (Attachment; ImpactStar, ImpactFlash, ImpactBolts, emit Top), BuffRing (gold 320-stud MeshPart disc). Only VoidEnemyController plays it; the timings are Archetypes.Sirenspine.Vfx. Every cast clones the parts it needs and destroys them after their longest life. Sirenspine buff​

Each pulse (at the Shockwave burst) sets DamageTakenMult, AttackRateMult and Buffed on every other void enemy within BuffRadius of the patrol circle's centre, and clears them on enemies that left the radius. When the Sirenspine dies or is removed, OnGone clears all three at once. CombatService.ApplyToModel multiplies NPC damage by DamageTakenMult (see Enemy Combat). The client draws a gold highlight and three tethers to the nearest Sirenspine's closest TetherBones tips while Buffed is set. The buff reach is measured from the centre of the patrol circle (not from the eel), so the escort keeps the buff for the whole lap; the ring and the hit still come from the eel.

Spawning​

The Void channel in Events Orchestrator rolls every 600 odometer studs (~21 s at cruise). VoidSwarmEvent.CanTrigger blocks every roll until the crew has finished any weapon research (a RangedWeapon or TrainWeapon node at level 1 or more) or the train has made its first jump to a new biome (VoidSwarmEvent.unlocked). After that it blocks rolls during a jump and passes with the current biome's Chance. Both conditions are for this run only (research is session-only). Start picks a pack by the biome's Weights, spawns it into an EnemyWave, and finishes when the wave clears or on a jump. There is no time limit. The wave is on the event's Janitor, so every end path despawns survivors.

Testing​

No test suite. In edit mode, each of these returns without an error (Command Bar):

loadstring(game.ReplicatedStorage.SharedModules.VoidEnemyMath.Source)().selfCheck()
loadstring(game.ReplicatedStorage.SharedModules.VoidEnemyRig.Source)().selfCheck()
loadstring(game.ServerStorage.SharedModules.VoidEnemyService.Source)().selfCheck()
loadstring(game.ServerStorage.SharedModules.Events.VoidSwarmEvent.Source)().selfCheck()
loadstring(game.ReplicatedStorage.SharedModules.VoidEnemyClips.Source)().selfCheck()
-- and each VoidEnemyBehaviors.<Id>.selfCheck()

In a playtest, type /swarm in chat for a Shatterbeak school, or /swarm Voltmaw for another pack (owner / rank 250+ only; packet ReqDevForceSwarm). On the server, require(game.ServerStorage.SharedModules.Events.VoidSwarmEvent).Force("Voltmaw") does the same. Shield damage needs a started train (Signals.SetTrainStarted:Fire(true)).

Team preview: tools/studio-previews/void-enemy-preview.luau runs the Void Cyst and Drifter in edit mode with the Studio looks (the Shatterbeak, Voltmaw and Sirenspine are animated looks: see them in a playtest); set PHASES and SHIELD_ONLINE at the top and paste it into the Command Bar.

Config surface​
