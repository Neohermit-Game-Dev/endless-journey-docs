AimPose

Holds an R15 rig's upper body steady and bends it at UpperTorso.Waist so the held tool's barrel line goes through an aim point. The legs walk; the torso, head, arms and gun are held still in the root part's frame (the walk animation's hip and waist sway is cancelled), and turn only to aim. Used on the client for player characters (AimPoseController) and on the server for armed pirates (EnemyBrain), so a pirate's real Muzzle points at its target.

The barrel is Handle.Muzzle (position) plus a Handle-space direction: the holding joint's C1.LookVector (Tool.Grip / the EnemyGrip weld; every gun's barrel, by the art rule). A tool with no barrel (throwables, Grabhook) uses the Handle (or its Muzzle) and the chest's forward line.

No feedback: the barrel is measured in UpperTorso space (the bend does not move it there), the twist/bend is solved in the steady frame, and the waist frame is set from this frame's animation pose. Call Step after the animation step (RunService.Stepped), or the cancel lags a frame and the body wobbles.

Twist and bend (radians) so the barrel line goes through target. All inputs are in the waist joint frame with no bend: muzzle/dir = the barrel as it sits unbent, target = the aim point. pivot (the shoulder) and share: the waist takes share of the pitch, the arms turn the rest about pivot. Starts from yaw/pitch; the result is clamped to AimPoseConfig.YawLimit / PitchLimit.

new​

</>
AimPose.new(rig: Model) → Pose?

A pose for rig, or nil when it has no UpperTorso.Waist on its LowerTorso. The Waist is a Motor6D (pirate rigs: the bend goes in C0) or, on player characters with upgraded avatar joints, an AnimationConstraint (the bend goes in its Attachment0.CFrame, the same place in the joint formula: UpperTorso = Attachment0.WorldCFrame * Transform * Attachment1.CFrame:Inverse()). LowerTorso.Root (either kind) gives the steady frame; without it the frame is the LowerTorso (sway not cancelled).

SetWeapon​

Sets the held tool: its Handle and the Handle-space barrel direction (Tool.Grip.LookVector for a gun; nil = aim the chest's forward line from the Handle, for throwables and Grabhook). nil handle = nothing held: the pose eases back to the plain animation. holdTarget / holdGrip: when an IKControl pins holdGrip to holdTarget (WeaponIK), the gun is measured where the IK puts it (the IK solves before the bend moves its target, so the hand itself is a frame late). holdIK: that IKControl. The arms then take part of the up/down aim (1 - WaistPitchShare): its Offset turns the hand about the right shoulder.

</>

Step​

One frame, after the animation step. aimPoint nil (or nothing held) eases back to the plain animation. rate = follow rate (an AimPoseConfig TurnRate). sample false holds the barrel sample and the waist pose (fire, throw and reload animations move the body and gun on purpose: they show).

Destroy​

</>
AimPose.Destroy(pose: Pose) → ()

Puts the waist back to its unbent joint frame.

selfCheck​

</>
AimPose.selfCheck() → boolean

Pure check of solve: the barrel line hits the target within 0.5 degrees for an offset barrel and a tilted barrel; a target behind clamps with no NaN.
