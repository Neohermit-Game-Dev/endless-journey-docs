Energy Cannon

A train-mounted twin-barrel plasma turret that a player mans and fires by holding the trigger. It is a tier 2 train weapon, next to the AutoCannon: a grid placeable (max 2 per train), crafted at the Workbench after the TrainWep_EnergyCannon research (the Burst Cannon needs that node). It fires cyan plasma bolts, 4 per second, the left and right barrels alternating.

Its role: it never needs ammo and never reloads, but it overheats. The AutoCannon hits a little harder over a long fight; the Energy Cannon shines in short bursts from cold.

The Energy Cannon runs on the same code as the Burst Cannon and the AutoCannon: ShellTurretServer on the server and TrainTurret/ShellWeapon on the client, each started with EnergyCannonConfig and the Energy Cannon packets. Mounting, seating, aim, the fire loop, unmanned Smart Cannon / Orbital Sentinel fire, Stash / Move prompts and the generic TurretGui work as described on those pages. Two optional modes of that shared code make it different: heat mode (on because HeatPerShot is set) and no-splash bolts (on because BlastRadius = 0). This page lists only what differs.

Packets: ReqMountEnergyCannon, ReqEnergyCannonTrigger, ReqEnergyCannonAim (unreliable), EnergyCannonFired (barrel index 1–2), EnergyCannonImpact (hit position). No reload packet, no ExplosionAt. See Networking Map.

Attributes: AimYaw, AimPitch, GunnerUserId, DisplayName ("ENERGY CANNON"), plus the heat set Heat (0..1, throttled: at most every 0.1 s and 0.01 change; 0 and 1 at once), Overheated, MaxHeat (1), PeakHeat (0.75). No Ammo / MagazineSize, so TurretGui shows its heat bar and no R hint.

Heat​

Each bolt adds HeatPerShot (1/32, less the Gun Crew perk's CannonHeatReduction). The bolt that reaches 1 still fires, then the cannon locks (Overheated). It keeps cooling at CoolPerSec (0.25/s) while locked and unlocks OverheatPenalty (1 s) after heat reaches 0, so a full lock lasts about 5 s. It only cools when no bolt fired within the last BurstPause: each tick fires first, then cools, so held fire never cools between bolts. A held trigger starts firing again by itself after the lock.

Unmanned fire (Smart Cannon / Orbital Sentinel) pays the same heat per bolt and stops at AutoStopHeat (0.9) until heat is down to AutoResumeHeat (0.5), so it never overheats.

The gunner's client runs the same heat model to predict its own bolts (it stops at overheat without waiting for the server) and resyncs from Heat / Overheated on mount and on every overheat edge.

Bolts​

FastCast2 spherecasts like the shells, but GravityScale = 0 (no drop), ShellSpeed 600 and no splash: a bolt damages only what it hits. FuseRadius (1.5) is kept as a small hit radius round enemy aim points, so enemies whose server parts cannot be ray-hit still take the bolt. An enemy takes TrainWeaponDamage.applyDirect, a rock RockService.Damage; the server then sends EnergyCannonImpact, which every client shows as a short cyan flash with the EnergyCannonHit sound.

Looks​

  • Bolt: client-only pooled neon tracer TracerColor cyan, TracerSize 0.3 × 0.3 × 5; muzzle flash FlashColor.
  • Barrels kick only a little (BarrelStroke 0.2, FoldIn 0.04 s, FoldOut 0.2 s).
  • HeatGlow: the GlowParts (CoilGlow, MuzzleGlow, PipeCore) and every Flow beam under them go HeatColors.cold (cyan) → warm (orange, heat 0.6) → hot (red, heat 1). While overheated they blink red and the pipe Flow beams stop. Driven by attribute changes only; parts that stream in late are picked up.
  • Every client plays EnergyCannonOverheat when Overheated turns on and EnergyCannonVent when it turns off.

Rig (template)​

The AutoCannon rig, restyled (same joint names and contract):

  • Base (anchored PrimaryPart, union) → Base.Yaw Motor6D → Body.Frame (union).
  • Body.Frame.Pitch Motor6D → Gun.GunBody (union: receiver, side energy pipes with bolted flanges and ball elbows, built-in monitor console).
  • Gun.Barrel_L / Gun.Barrel_R (unions: thick coil body, emitter collar, three flared prongs) each with a Slide Motor6D and a MuzzleTip attachment at (±0.55, 0.30, −9.35) in the pitch-joint frame (= MuzzleTips).
  • Glow and glass details are plain Parts welded to their union (no collision, query, touch or shadow): CoilGlow ×2, MuzzleGlow ×2 (emitter core), PipeGlass ×2, PipeCore ×2 (each with a Flow beam between FlowRear / FlowFront), MonitorScreen (SurfaceGui Display, static crosshair), ConsoleGlow.
  • The side pipes clear the Frame's pitch cheeks (discs of radius 1.4 round the pivot); keep anything that bridges the receiver and the outside more than 1.65 studs from the pivot.

Check the math in the Command Bar: loadstring(game.ReplicatedStorage.SharedModules.EnergyCannonMath.Source)().selfCheck().

Spread​

The server rolls every shell (manned and unmanned) inside a cone of SpreadBase + bloom after the aim-cone clamp (ShellTurretServer, CrosshairMath, WeaponValidation.applySpread). Each shot adds SpreadPerShot (capped at SpreadMax); the bloom decays at SpreadRecovery degrees/s. The gunner client mirrors the bloom with its predicted shots and the TurretGui crosshair shows that cone (see Crosshair).
