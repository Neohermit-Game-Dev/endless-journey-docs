Paid Revive

In the Game Instance a death is final until the player revives. Every revive costs 99 R$ (one Developer Product, no cap). Once per run (one reserved server) a player may instead watch a rewarded video ad, but only when an ad is ready right now. A down player can also spectate or go back to the lobby. When the whole crew is down a shared countdown ends the run, and the Run Results screen takes over.

Down players earn nothing (kill XP and coins, party XP, party quest progress, jump arrival rewards) and do not vote on the Jump Drive.

At a glance​

LayerFileRole
Sharedsrc/ReplicatedStorage/SharedModules/ReviveRules.luauPure rules: Status, Phase, allDown, countdownPhase, canBuy, canAd, shouldSpendCredit (Studio self-check at the bottom)
Configsrc/ReplicatedStorage/SharedModules/Configs/ReviveConfig.luauProduct id, countdown and hold seconds, store name, ad placement, GUI timings
Serversrc/ServerStorage/SharedModules/ReviveState.luauWho is down right now. Read by reward and vote code (LivingPlayers, IsAlive). Also mapped into the Lobby (XPManager / QuestManager require it; nobody is down there)
Serversrc/ServerStorage/SharedModules/ReviveCredits.luauSaved revive credits (PlayerRevive ProfileStore): receipt idempotency, Grant, TakeOne
Serversrc/ServerStorage/SharedModules/ReviveService.luauSpawn, death, revive, purchase prompt, rewarded ad, receipts, crew countdown
Serversrc/ServerScriptService/ReviveSystemLoader.server.luauReviveCredits.Initialize(), ReviveService.Initialize(), then ReceiptRouter.Start() (the Game Instance's one ProcessReceipt)

Packets: ReqReviveBuy, ReqReviveAd, ReviveStateSync — see Networking Map Signals: OnPlayerDownChanged — see Signals Map

How it works​

ReviveService.Initialize sets Players.CharacterAutoLoads = false and spawns each joining player with LoadCharacter. On a character's Humanoid.Died it:

  1. captures the player's Tools (SessionState.CaptureTools),
  2. sets the status to Down and ReviveState.SetDown(player, true),
  3. sends ReviveStateSync,
  4. spends a saved credit at once if the player has one (status UsingCredit).

A revive shows Reviving / UsingCredit for REVIVE_DELAY_SECONDS (1 s), then calls LoadCharacter (normal spawn, full health from HealthManager's CharacterAdded reset) and puts the captured Tools back with SessionState.RestoreTools.

Every receipt becomes a saved credit first​

A Robux purchase and a rewarded ad grant the same "Revive" product. Its ProcessReceipt handler is ReviveCredits.Grant: +1 credit once per PurchaseId, PurchaseGranted only after a save that contains the id (the QuestManager.settle pattern). Only then does ReviveService spend the credit on the down player. If the player is not down any more, or the run is over, the credit stays saved and revives them on their next death, in any run.

The grant comes only from the receipt, never from ShowRewardedVideoAdAsync's result or PromptProductPurchaseFinished. An ad receipt is recognised by receipt.ProductPurchaseChannel == Enum.ProductPurchaseChannel.AdReward and marks the run's ad as used.

sequenceDiagram
    participant C as Client
    participant S as ReviveService
    participant M as MarketplaceService
    participant P as ReviveCredits
    S->>C: ReviveStateSync(Down)
    C->>S: ReqReviveBuy
    S->>C: ReviveStateSync(Buying)
    S->>M: PromptProductPurchase
    M-->>S: ProcessReceipt (ReceiptRouter)
    S->>P: Grant: +1 credit, save
    P-->>S: saved → onGranted
    S->>P: TakeOne
    S->>C: ReviveStateSync(Reviving)
    Note over S: 1 s, then LoadCharacter + RestoreTools
    S->>C: ReviveStateSync(Alive)

A down player never keeps a saved credit: besides the death, the rejoin and the receipt, a 1 s heartbeat spends one for any present Down player. That catches a receipt whose save took longer than 20 s (Roblox retries it, and the retry only answers PurchaseGranted) and a credits profile that loaded after the death.

The ad flow is the same with ReqReviveAd → AdPlaying → AdService:ShowRewardedVideoAdAsync(player, CreateAdRewardFromDevProductId(PRODUCT_ID)). A finished prompt or ad whose receipt has not landed keeps the status (and the countdown hold) for up to 30 s, then returns to Down.

Rejoin while down​

State is keyed by UserId and lives for the server's life. A player who leaves during the 1 s "REVIVING…" step already paid, so the revive finishes when they rejoin. A down player who disconnects and rejoins is not spawned: they come back Down on the menu, with the same "ad used" flag and the Tools captured at death. The rejoin grace health provider skips a health of 0, so a later revive is never killed by a restored 0.

Studio stand-ins​

While ReviveConfig.PRODUCT_ID == 0 there is no product. In Studio ReqReviveBuy revives at once and ReqReviveAd marks the ad used and revives at once, each with a warning. Outside Studio both do nothing.

All-down countdown​

ReviveService re-checks the crew on every status change, on every leave, and once a second:

  • present = players in the server; down = present players whose status is not Alive;
  • graceAlive = players seen this run who are disconnected, still in rejoin grace (SessionState.HasGrace) and were alive.

When ReviveRules.allDown(present, down, graceAlive) turns true, crewEndsAt = now + ALL_DOWN_SECONDS (20 s, server time) and every client gets it in ReviveStateSync. A revive (or a living player joining) clears it.

Each second ReviveRules.countdownPhase moves the countdown on:

PhaseWhen
Runningbefore crewEndsAt
Holdat 0 while a purchase prompt, ad or revive is in flight, for at most HOLD_MAX_SECONDS (30 s). holdBy names the first such player
Endotherwise

At End the run is over: every status becomes RunOver (ReviveService.MarkRunOver, idempotent), then TrainGameOver.Trigger(TrainGameOverReason.ALL_DOWN). RunEndService owns the rest: payout, the results screen, and SessionCoordinator.EndRunToLobby() after RunRewardConfig.RESULTS_SECONDS (see Run Results). A hull breach or Leviathan strike calls MarkRunOver first and then kills every living player; their deaths see RunOver and start nothing. ReviveService also counts each down and each revive into RunStats. After the end nothing revives; a receipt that still lands becomes a saved credit for the next run, and a credit taken for a revive that had not loaded yet is given back (ReviveCredits.GiveBack). If the lobby teleport fails, the player is kicked with a message instead of waiting on "GOING TO THE LOBBY…" (not in Studio, where teleports always fail). A player who rejoins after the end gets no character; RunEndService pays them and shows the results inside the results window, or bounces only that player after it.

Only the all-down end works this way. A hull breach or a Leviathan strike does not change who is down.

Client​

ReviveGuiController (StarterPlayerScripts/ReviveGuiController/) binds three Studio-built ScreenGuis (all ResetOnSpawn = false, Enabled = false in StarterGui) and draws only what ReviveStateSync says:

ScreenGuiDisplayOrderHolds
ReviveDimGui-2 (below NovaChat's -1)Dim — menu only, so the chat stays readable over it
ReviveGui20RevivePanel (menu) and SpectateBar
CrewDownGui21CountdownBanner — shown while the crew countdown runs or holds
  • Only the revive GUIs while down. HudBlackout (ReplicatedStorage/SharedModules, shared with the results screen: Set(owner, hide), hidden while any owner hides) records every other enabled PlayerGui ScreenGui, turns it off (and off again if anything turns it on or a new one appears), and turns exactly those back on at revive. Satchel's hotbar and the TopbarPlus icons use their own switches. NovaChat and the Roblox topbar stay (NovaChat follows TopbarEnabled). The cursor is freed with Signals.RequestMouseIcon(true, "Revive").
  • Run over. At RunOver the controller treats the player as not down: every revive GUI turns off, spectating stops, and it releases its HudBlackout owner and cursor request. The Run Results screen takes over (there is no "RUN OVER" copy).
  • Reset Character is off (SetCore("ResetButtonCallback", false), retried until the CoreGui accepts it): a free death would skip the paid revive.
  • Ad button shows only when the run's ad is unused and AdService:GetAdAvailabilityNowAsync(RewardedVideo) returned IsAvailable when the menu opened. An ad that did not finish (status back from AdPlaying to Down) triggers a new check, because IsAvailable is good for one ad play only. Any other result shows Robux only. The visible ad button is registered with RegisterAdOpportunityAsync.
  • Price comes from MarketplaceService:GetProductInfo(PRODUCT_ID, Product).PriceInRobux, written after the Robux glyph utf8.char(0xE002) (as in TechTree and QuestBoard). No price shows while PRODUCT_ID is 0, the call fails, or the product is not on sale. With regional pricing on, the price is the viewer's regional price (99 R$ is the default).
  • Lobby asks first: the first tap swaps in "LEAVE THE RUN?" + YES / NO; NO or LOBBY_CONFIRM_SECONDS (5 s) goes back; YES fires Signals.LeaveToLobbyRequested.
  • Spectate (Spectate.luau) cycles the other living players (Prev / Next, Q / E, gamepad L1 / R1) and fires Signals.RequestSpectateTarget; with nobody up the camera watches the train core (TrainConfig.CORE_TAG). The bar shows the target's head shot, name, "WATCHING · n OF m UP" and a health bar (orange under 30%).
  • Timer is computed every frame from crewEndsAt - workspace:GetServerTimeNow(), not sent each second.
  • Scale. UIScale = min(W/1920, H/1022); under 0.68 the compact layout (authored at 844×332, scale clamped to 0.75–1.5) is applied: the panel turns into a 780×252 row anchored to the bottom, the banner drops its second line and the bar shrinks to 68 high (LOBBY becomes icon-only).
  • Rejoin while down. StarterGui is copied into PlayerGui only on spawn, and a down player is not spawned, so the controller clones the three GUIs itself after 5 s and destroys the duplicate the next spawn brings.

Not yet tested​

  • A live rewarded ad and a real Robux purchase (needs the product id and ad eligibility).
  • A multi-player all-down, the hold with a real purchase prompt, and spectating another player.
  • Rejoin while down, and rejoin during the 1 s "REVIVING…" step (needs a reserved server).
  • A receipt whose save takes longer than 20 s (the heartbeat spend was tested with a credit granted outside the receipt path).
  • The lobby teleport at run end (Studio cannot teleport).

Configuration​

See ReviveConfig in the Config Map.

Gotchas​

  • One ProcessReceipt per server. The Game Instance now starts ReceiptRouter in ReviveSystemLoader, with only the Revive product registered. Every other product still returns NotProcessedYet here, so Roblox retries it in the Lobby. A Revive receipt that reaches the Lobby is retried in the next run.
  • Characters do not respawn by themselves. Anything that expects an automatic respawn after death must go through ReviveService.
